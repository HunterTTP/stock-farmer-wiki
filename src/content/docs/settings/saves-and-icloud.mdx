There is no account. Each farm is a save on the device, and on iOS 26 and later the farms also sync through your own iCloud.

## Farms and save slots

The game keeps up to 10 farms, one per save slot. Each farm saves by itself as you play, and again when the game goes to the background.

- **Load Game** is in Settings. It saves the farm, closes it and opens the list of farms.
- The list shows the farms most recently played first. Each shows its name, Level, money, play time and when it was last played.
- Tap a farm to open it.
- **New Farm** is at the bottom of the list. It asks for a name, then starts a farm in the first free slot. A first launch with no farms opens New Game, which asks for a name. See [New farm](../../world/new-farm/).
- At 10 farms New Farm is replaced by a note to delete a farm before starting another.
- Two farms cannot share a name, whatever the capitals or spaces.

### Farm options

Each farm in the list has an options button with these choices.

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Rename | Changes the farm's name. |
| Restore Backup | Brings back an earlier copy of the farm. Each farm keeps up to three backups: the save before the latest, one from at least ten minutes earlier, and the last save of the day before. A copy that lost out when syncing is kept here too. |
| Delete | Removes the farm after a confirmation. This cannot be undone. |
| Properties | Shows the farm's seed and the game version that last saved it. |

## iCloud sync

On iOS 26 and later the farms sync through your own iCloud, and a farm started on one device carries on on another. The game always plays from the copy on the device, and iCloud never holds up play.

- It syncs when the game opens, when it comes back from the background, from the cloud button on the Load Game list, and regularly while you play.
- Without a connection, the farm saves on the device as usual and syncs once the connection is back.
- The cloud button on the Load Game list shows the state, and tapping it syncs now and explains any problem.
- Before iOS 26, or with iCloud Drive off for the game, farms stay on the device.

### Banners over the open farm

The farm you are playing never changes by itself. When iCloud has another copy of it, a banner appears.

| Banner | Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Newer save from a device or from iCloud, with how long ago | Load | Opens the newer save. The copy it replaces goes to Restore Backup. |
| This farm was also played on another device | Choose | Closes the farm and opens Choose a Version in the farm list. |

The close button hides a banner for that copy until a different one arrives. Only one banner shows at a time.

### Choose a Version

When a farm was played on two devices and each copy has progress the other lacks, the game asks which to keep.

- The screen shows both copies side by side with Level, money, play time, day and device.
- It marks the copy with more progress.
- Keep one copy with its button. The copy not kept is saved in Restore Backup.
- Decide Later returns to the farm list.

### When iCloud cannot be reached

- Two failed syncs in a row show a message that iCloud could not be reached and that the farm is saved on the device. The game tries again later.
- States that need you, such as being signed out of iCloud, are explained when you tap the cloud button.
- Playing is never interrupted.

### A farm from a newer version

A farm saved by a newer version of the game is left as it is and is not opened. The cloud button explains that the game needs an update. Update the game to play it.

If a farm arrives from iCloud and all 10 slots are full, it is kept safe and waits until a slot is free.

## Update prompt

A newer version of the game on TestFlight or the App Store is announced once.

1. When the farm is free of menus, a prompt says an update is available and gives the new and installed version numbers.
2. Update opens TestFlight or the App Store.
3. Ignore closes the prompt for that version. A newer version prompts again.
4. After an Ignore, a yellow "!" sits between the money and the Level for as long as the update is available. Tapping it opens the prompt again.

The game checks for an update when it opens and when you come back to it after at least 6 hours.
