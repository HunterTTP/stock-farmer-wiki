Settings is a button in the ring that opens from the tool circle. See [Tools](../../tools/#the-tool-ring).

## Settings menu

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Screenshot Farm | Saves a picture of the whole farm to Photos. The picture size is set in [Timelapse](../timelapse/). |
| Timelapse | Records the farm at set times each day. See [Timelapse](../timelapse/). |
| Load Game | Saves the farm and opens the list of farms. See [Saves and iCloud](../saves-and-icloud/). |
| Sound | Volume sliders and the music player. |
| Graphics | What is drawn on screen. |
| Gameplay | Controls, the tutorial and tips. |
| About | Changelog, Credits and Privacy. |

Settings are stored on the device. They are the same for every farm, except the tutorial switch, which belongs to each farm.

## Sound

The Sound page opens with the Now Playing card, followed by the volume sliders.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Now Playing card | Shows the song playing with its artist, and has Previous, Play or Pause, and Next buttons. See [Music and sound](../music-and-sound/). |
| Music | Volume of the music, 0% to 100%. At 0% the card shows Music off. |
| Master | Volume that every sound follows. |
| Effects | Volume of buttons, menus, planting, building, fishing, cooking and animal sounds. |
| Ambience | Volume of rain, thunder, crickets, birdsong, the midnight chime and the season change sound. |

There are no on or off switches for sound. A volume of 0% turns that kind of sound off. Letting go of a slider plays a short sample of its sound.

## Graphics

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hide Grid Lines Beyond | A slider from 0× to 8× in steps of 0.5×. Grid lines fade as you zoom out and are gone beyond this zoom. At 0× they are always hidden and at 8× they always show. Starts at 2×. |
| Show Money | Shows or hides the money panel. |
| Show Level | Shows or hides the Level panel. |
| Christmas Lights | Opens Power, Pattern and All Year. See [Lanterns and lights](../../decorations/lanterns-and-lights/). |
| Weather effects | Draws weather over the farm. Off removes rain, snow, falling leaves and petals, curls of wind and the tint. Animals still shelter and the rain sound still plays. On by default. |
| Clouds | Shows the drifting clouds and their shadows over the farm (see [Weather](../../world/weather/#floating-clouds)). On by default. |

## Gameplay

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Tool Offset | How far up and to the right of the dragged tool circle a tool works. 0 is right under the circle. A slider from 0 pt to 200 pt, starting at 16 pt, with a Reset button. |
| Open Tool Menu on Select | When on, picking a tool opens its first list at once. On by default. The tutorial always opens it. |
| Tutorial | Shows or hides this farm's tutorial and the tips after it. |
| Replay Tutorial | Starts the tutorial again from its first step. Shown once the tutorial is complete. |

## Reset to Defaults

Sound, Graphics, Christmas Lights and Gameplay each end with a Reset to Defaults button. It asks first, then puts every setting on that page back to how the game first came. On the Sound page it also restores the song order. See [Music and sound](../music-and-sound/).

## About

| Row | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Changelog | Every change, one card per real-world day, newest first. It has a search field, a calendar button to show one day or a range of days, and Newer and Older buttons for pages of seven days. |
| Credits | Who made the game's art, music and sounds, in cards for Game, Art, Music, Sound and Code. A title that has a page opens it, and a licence name opens its text. |
| Privacy | The privacy policy, which is stored in the game and opens without a connection. |

The line under the rows names the installed version. Holding a finger on it offers Copy Version.
