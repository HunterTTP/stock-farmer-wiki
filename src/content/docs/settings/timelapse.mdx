Settings → Timelapse takes a picture of the whole farm at chosen times of the in-game day. The pictures can be played back as a timelapse or exported as a video. Each farm has its own capture times and pictures.

## How it records

- A picture is taken each time the farm clock reaches a capture time while you play.
- The default is one capture time at 9 AM, with recording on.
- If the clock jumps past several capture times at once, only the latest is taken.
- Closing and reopening the farm, changing the times or switching recording on does not make up for missed pictures.
- A capture time taken once in a day is not taken again that day.
- Pictures stay in the game until you delete them. They are not in the Photos app.

## Settings on the Timelapse screen

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Screenshots | The number of pictures taken. |
| Saved images | The room the pictures take. |
| Capture times | One to three times of day, each set by hour and minute. Add Time adds another four hours after the latest. Remove Time deletes one. A farm always keeps at least one. Times stay in order and no two are alike. |
| Picture size | One size for the timelapse and for Screenshot Farm: Smallest, Small, Medium, Large or Largest. Medium is the default. The screen estimates the room one picture and one day take. Pictures already taken keep their size. |
| Crossfade Transitions | Blends from one picture to the next in playback. Off, the pictures change at once. On by default. |
| Enable Timelapse | Turns recording on or off. Turning it off keeps the pictures and playback. |

## Playing and exporting

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Play Timelapse | Plays the pictures from oldest to newest, one a second, and stops at the end. The viewer shows the day and time of the picture and has Play or Pause, a scrubber to move through the pictures, and an X to close. Pinch to zoom, drag to move and double tap to zoom in or back out. |
| Export Video | Makes an MP4 video of the pictures, oldest first, and shows its progress. Share Video then appears, and Save Video in the share sheet adds it to Photos. |
| Clear Timelapse | Asks first, then deletes all the farm's pictures. Capture times and settings stay. |

Play Timelapse and Export Video are greyed out until at least one picture exists. Deleting a farm deletes its pictures too.
