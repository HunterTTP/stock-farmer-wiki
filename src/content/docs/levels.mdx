Your level decides what you can plant, build and craft, and how much farmland you can have. XP raises your level.

- You start at level 1 with 0 XP.
- Selling crops, fish and goods earns gold. Harvesting crops, collecting animal produce, collecting finished workshop batches, fishing, catching butterflies and fireflies, and finishing quests earn XP.
- The top level is <Fact path="world.maxLevel" />, where Coffee unlocks. No more XP is earned there, and quests, workshops, animals, fish and butterflies pay none.
- XP is never lost. Several levels can be gained at once.

## What earns XP

| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Harvest a crop tile with the Scythe | The crop's XP at your level (always at least 1 XP; see [Crops](../farming/crops/)) |
| Pick a fruit basket with the Bucket | The tree's XP at your level (see [Fruit Trees](../farming/fruit-trees/)) |
| Collect what an animal made | Static XP by animal kind (see below) |
| Collect a finished workshop batch | 8 XP per minute of craft time (1.25× for two-step recipes) |
| Land a fish | Depends on rarity, weight and level |
| Catch a butterfly or a wild firefly with the Net | Depends on color |
| Complete a Market Stand quest | A share of the level's XP |

### Crops and fruit

- Through level 9, a crop earns its unlock level plus one (Wheat 1, Carrot 3, Potato 4, up to Broccoli 10), and a fruit tree basket earns one and a half times its level's crop XP, rounded.
- From level 10 to level 22, crop XP gradually tapers down by grow time (always staying at least 1 XP) so animals, Market Stand quests and workshops become the fastest way to level up while crops stay essential for gold, quest targets and workshop ingredients.
- A second crop from a tool upgrade earns no XP.
- The [Tractor](../buildings/tractor/) earns the XP when its load is stored or sold.

### Animal produce

Each animal pays a fixed XP per item collected (3.2 XP per minute of its base interval), so upgrading homes and tending animals pays steady XP at every level. Hand-feeding earns no direct XP, while Well Fed speeds production by 25%.

A second item from a tool upgrade earns no XP. See [Animals](../animals/).

### Workshop recipes

Collecting a finished workshop batch pays 8 XP per minute of craft time, multiplied by 1.25 for two-step recipes made from another crafted good (such as Bread, Furniture, Fine Cloth and Aged Cheese). Starting a batch pays no XP until it is collected.

### Fish, butterflies and fireflies

These earn a share of the XP needed to leave your level.

| Catch | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Fish | XP to leave the level ÷ divisor (<Fact path="fish.xp.levelDivisor" /> through level 15, climbing by 30 per level to 700 at level 29+) × rarity multiplier (1, 2, 4, 7, 10) × weight factor (<Fact path="fish.weight.lightestXPFactor" /> to <Fact path="fish.weight.heaviestXPFactor" />) |
| Butterfly or wild firefly | XP to leave the level ÷ <Fact path="world.butterflies.xpLevelDivisor" /> × color multiplier (1, 2, 4, 7, 10, or <Fact path="world.butterflies.shiftingXPMultiplier" /> for Shifting) |

- Each catch earns at least 1 XP. One you let go or set free earns nothing when caught again.
- At level 10, which takes 3,696 XP to leave, an average Common fish earns about 12 XP.
- See [Fishing](../gathering/fishing/#xp-for-fish) and [Butterflies and Fireflies](../gathering/butterflies-and-fireflies/#xp).

### Quests

- Through level 9, a quest pays <Fact path="quests.smallRewardMinPercent" />% to <Fact path="quests.smallRewardMaxPercent" />% of the XP needed to leave your level (<Fact path="quests.largeRewardMinPercent" />% to <Fact path="quests.largeRewardMaxPercent" />% on the upgraded stand).
- From level 10 on, quests scale to 2% to 6% on the Market Stand and 8.5% to 16.5% on the upgraded stand, favoring animal, workshop, ore and stock objectives. See [Quests](../quests/#xp-rewards).

## How level XP is worked out

The XP needed to leave a level is <Fact path="world.harvestsPerLevel" /> harvests of a full field of the newest crop:

> XP to leave a level = <Fact path="world.harvestsPerLevel" /> × farmland tiles at that level × XP of the crop that unlocks at that level

- Level 1: 4 tiles × 3 harvests × 1 XP (Wheat) = 12 XP.
- Level 2: 8 tiles × 3 × 3 XP (Carrot) = 72 XP.
- Level 3: 14 tiles × 3 × 4 XP (Potato) = 168 XP.
- At a level where a fruit tree unlocks, the tree's plain XP is used, without the extra half. Level 4 (Apple Tree) is 22 tiles × 3 × 5 XP = 330 XP.

## Farmland per level

- You start with <Fact path="world.startingFarmland" /> farmland tiles.
- Levels <Fact path="terrain.farmlandFirstUnlockLevel" /> to <Fact path="terrain.farmlandDoubleToLevel" /> each add twice their number in tiles.
- Level <Fact path="terrain.farmlandSingleFromLevel" /> and above each add as many tiles as their number.
- See [Farmland and Water](../farming/farmland-and-water/).

## Leveling up

- A level-up unlocks everything listed for the new level in the level table at once.
- Farmland, crops, fruit trees, buildings, recipes, tool and home upgrades, the levels where rarer fish bite more often, and stock quests all unlock by level. Decorations never do: they need only gold and materials.
