The House opens the [Stock Market](../../stock-market/). Tapping it, with any tool selected, opens the Stock Market menu instead of a building menu.

<Infobox kind="building" id="house1" />

- Only one House can be built, in either form.
- The House holds no stocks. If it is destroyed, the stocks you own stay with the farm and are there again once a House is rebuilt.
- A rebuilt House is the plain House again, with the lower stock limit. Buy the Luxury upgrade again to raise it.
- The chimney always smokes, in both forms. At night the windows glow.

## Luxury House

The Luxury House raises the number of different stocks you can own.

| | House | Luxury House |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Different stocks you can own | <Fact path="home-upgrades.house.stockSlotsBefore" /> | <Fact path="home-upgrades.house.stockSlotsAfter" /> |
| Size (tiles) | <Fact path="buildings.house1.width" />×<Fact path="buildings.house1.height" /> | <Fact path="home-upgrades.house.width" />×<Fact path="home-upgrades.house.height" /> |

- The Luxury House is not sold in the Build tool. The House is upgraded in place.
- Unlocks at level <Fact path="home-upgrades.house.unlockLevel" />.
- Costs <Fact path="home-upgrades.house.cost" as="money" />.
- The Luxury House is taller than the House, so it needs free ground for its larger size. If there is no room, move the House first (see [Placing and moving](../placing-and-moving/)).

To upgrade:

1. Tap the House to open the Stock Market menu.
2. Tap Upgrade, under Buy Stock. The button needs an internet connection, like the rest of the Stock Market.
3. A page explains the upgrade and its cost. Tap Upgrade with the price. Before the unlock level it shows the level needed instead.
