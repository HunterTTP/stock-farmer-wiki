The House opens the [Stock Market](../../stock-market/).

<Infobox kind="building" id="house1" />

- Only one House can be built, in either form.
- The House holds no stocks. If it is destroyed, the stocks you own stay with the farm and are there again once a House is rebuilt.
- A rebuilt House is the plain House again, with the lower stock limit.

## Luxury House

The Luxury House is an upgrade of the House in place. It raises the number of different stocks you can own.

- The Luxury House is taller, so it needs free ground for its larger size.
- Buying the upgrade needs an internet connection, like the rest of the Stock Market.
