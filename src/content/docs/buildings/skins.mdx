A skin changes how a building looks and nothing else. Skins are bought with gold and put on in the building's own menu, on its Skins tab. The Market Stand's menu has Quests, Supplies and Skins tabs, and the House's has Stock Market and Skins. Every other building's menu has Info and Skins tabs.

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | Animated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 5 times the building's cost, at least 1,250 gold | No |
| Rare | 12.5 times the cost, at least 2,500 gold | Paw Prints |
| Epic | 25 times the cost, at least 5,000 gold | Always |
| Legendary | 50 times the cost, at least 7,500 gold | Always |

- Prices are rounded to the nearest 500 gold below 10,000 gold, to 1,000 gold below 100,000 gold and to 5,000 gold above that.
- The upgraded Market Stand and the Luxury House are priced from their upgraded cost.
- Gold Leaf is a flat <Fact path="market.goldLeafPrice" as="money" />.

## Common skins

Every building comes in <Fact path="market.basicColours" /> plain colours, and both Market Stands also have Classic Yellow. The Trunk has the same <Fact path="market.basicColours" /> colours.

| Legendary | Ember, Hoarfrost, Gold Leaf, Galaxy Swirl, Prismatic, Pixie Dust, Duck Parade, Molten Chrome |

- Haunted, Crystal Geode, Neon Sign, Ghost Flame, Meteor Shower, Galaxy Swirl and Pixie Dust also glow at night.

## Buying and equipping

- A skin can be bought only while a building of that kind stands on the farm.
- A skin is bought once for each kind of building. Every building of that kind can then wear it, one skin at a time.
- Tapping a skin opens a preview with the four seasons, Day and Night, and a swipe to the next skin. After buying, a skin can be equipped at once or later. Equipping puts it on that one building.
- A building keeps its skin when it is moved or upgraded.
- Destroying a building drops its skin from that building. The skins you bought stay yours.
- A skin a building no longer sells falls back to its original look.
- The Market Stand and its upgrade share their skins, and so do the House and the Luxury House.

## The Trunk

The Trunk (see [Decorations](../../decorations/)) has its own menu with Info and Skins tabs, and 35 skins: the <Fact path="market.basicColours" /> colours, Paw Prints, ten Epic and eight Legendary skins, priced like the Pet House's. Its lid opens in the skin's colours and stays open while its menu is up.
