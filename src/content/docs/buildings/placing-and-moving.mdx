## Placing a building

1. Pick the building in the Build tool's dropdown (see [Buildings](../)).
2. Tap the farm. The building appears as a preview.
3. Move it to the spot you want.
4. Tap Build. The button shows the price.

- The preview glows green where the building fits and red where it does not.
- The tag hanging under the preview names the building and says "Fits here" or why it cannot go there. Build is greyed out while it cannot.
- Building charges its money, logs and stones. Moving is free.
- While a preview is out, taps on the farm do nothing except on the handles around the preview, and the camera still pans and zooms.
- Cancel puts the preview away.

Reasons a spot is refused:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not enough space | Something is in the way of the ground the building stands on. |
| Needs a free tile in front | A pet house or animal home has no free tile below it. |
| That would block the front of a pet house or coop | Another thing would cover the front tile of a home. |
| You can only place one (or You can place at most) | The limit for that building is reached. |
| Need, followed by a price | Not enough money. |
| Need a number of logs or stones in storage | Not enough materials. |
| A crop here is ready to harvest | A planted crop is on the ground. A crop that is still growing says how long is left. |

## Ground a building needs

- Only the bottom row of a building is the ground it stands on. The rest of its picture rises over the tiles behind it.
- The ground cannot be water, a rock, a fence, the ground of a tree or decoration, a planted crop or fruit tree, or the ground of another building.
- Bare farmland under a building is turned into ground for the building.
- Animals standing there are moved to a free tile.
- The upper rows of a building's picture may overlap trees, fences and decorations that stand behind it.
- The upper part of a building may rise past the back edge of the map, so a Silo can stand on the back row. The Tractor stays fully inside the map.
- Pet houses and animal homes need the tile in front of them to stay clear: plain grass, farmland or a crop, with no building, water, fence, rock, tree or decoration on it. Nothing placed later may cover it either.

## Moving things by holding

Hold a finger on any of these to pick it up:

- a building
- a fruit tree
- a decoration, including a sprinkler
- a tree you planted yourself (wild trees cannot be moved)

The thing is picked up where it stands and the same tag appears under it.

- Drag the thing itself, or one of the arrow handles on its sides, to move it. It snaps a tile at a time. The handle being dragged turns gold.
- Tap a handle to move one tile.
- Tap Place to put it down. Tap Cancel to leave it where it was.
- Moving costs nothing and keeps everything about it: its look, its animals, its level, its work, a tree's growth. A moved sprinkler sprays again at once.
- A Tractor cannot be moved while it is driving.

## Nudge

The Nudge button on the tag opens a pad of nine positions. It moves only the picture of a building, fruit tree, tree or decoration, by a quarter of a tile toward a side or a corner. The tiles it covers stay where they are.

- The pad starts at the position the thing already has, the middle for a new one.
- The position is kept when you tap Place and dropped on Cancel.
- Tap Nudge again, or the farm, to close the pad.

## Destroying

Hold a building until it is picked up, then tap the bin on the tag. The tag asks "Destroy?" with Keep and Destroy.

- A destroyed building gives back <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its price and of each material, rounded to whole items. The Pet House costs no money, so it gives back materials only.
- A decoration gives back <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its price. A tree you planted gives back <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of what it cost to plant. A fruit tree gives back no money.
- A Shed or Silo can only be destroyed while the remaining storage still holds everything stored. Otherwise the game says how many items to empty first.
- A destroyed workshop loses any batch in progress. An animal home loses its level.
- Wild trees come down with the Axe and give logs. The Axe also takes down decorations and fences into storage (see [Storage and selling](../../storage-and-selling/)).
