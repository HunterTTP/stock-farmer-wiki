Buildings cost money plus logs and stones from Storage, and each unlocks at a player level.

## All buildings

<DataTable name="buildings" />

- **Materials** come from Storage. The Pet House costs no money and takes one Copper Ore besides its logs and stones.
- **Size** is width × height in tiles. Only the bottom row is ground the building stands on.
- **Most you can build:** one of most buildings. The Chicken Coop, Shed and Silo allow <Fact path="buildings.chickenCoop.limit" /> each, and the Pet House one for every kind of pet.
- Level unlocks are listed on the [Levels](../levels/) page.
- The Trunk is built from the same Build list, after the Pet House (see [Decorations](../decorations/)).
- Every building can wear bought skins (see [Skins](skins/)).

- A placed building keeps its look. To change it, destroy it and build it again.

## Placing

A building's ground is its bottom row of tiles. The rest of its picture rises over the tiles behind it.

- The ground cannot be water, a rock, a fence, an egg, a planted crop or fruit tree, or the ground of a tree, decoration or another building.
- Bare farmland under a building becomes ground for it. Animals standing there are moved to a free tile.
- The upper rows of a building may overlap trees, fences and decorations behind it, and may rise past the back edge of the map. The Tractor stays fully inside the map.
- The Pet House and every animal home need the tile in front of them to stay plain ground (grass, farmland or a crop). Nothing placed later may cover it, and no water may be laid there.
- Building charges its money, logs and stones when it is placed.

## Moving

Buildings, fruit trees, decorations, sprinklers and trees you planted can be picked up and moved. Wild trees cannot.

- Moving is free and keeps everything: the look, the animals, the level, the work in progress, a tree's growth.
- Animals follow their home to its new spot.
- A Tractor cannot be moved while it is driving.

## Destroying

| Thing | Gives back |
| --- | --- |
| Building | <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its price and of each material, rounded to whole items |
| Decoration | <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its price and of each material |
| Tree you planted | <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of what it cost to plant |
| Fruit tree | Nothing |

- A Shed or Silo can only be destroyed while the rest of Storage still holds everything stored.
- A destroyed workshop loses any batch in progress. A destroyed animal home loses its level and its animals.
- A Tractor cannot be destroyed while it is driving or carrying a load.
