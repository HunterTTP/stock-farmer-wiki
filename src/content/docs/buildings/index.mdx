Buildings are made with the Build tool. Each one costs money, logs and stones from storage, and unlocks at a player level.

## All buildings

<DataTable name="buildings" />

- **Cost** is paid in money when you confirm the spot.
- **Materials** are taken from storage. The Pet House costs no money and takes one Copper Ore besides logs and stones.
- **Size** is width × height in tiles. Only the bottom row of a building is ground it stands on (see [Placing and moving](placing-and-moving/)).
- **Most you can build** is the limit for that building. Most buildings allow one. The Chicken Coop, Shed and Silo allow <Fact path="buildings.chickenCoop.limit" /> each, and the Pet House allows one for every kind of pet in the game.
- Level unlocks are listed on the [Levels](../levels/) page.

## Build tool

1. Tap the circle in the bottom-left corner and pick Build.
2. Open the building dropdown beside the circle and pick a building.
3. Tap the farm. A preview appears. Move it, then confirm with the Build button, which shows the price.

In the dropdown:

- Each row shows its money price and its logs and stones. A number is green when storage has enough and red when it does not.
- A locked building reads "Unlocks at Lv" and its level.
- A building with a limit shows how many are placed out of how many are allowed, such as Shed (2/4). A row at its limit is greyed out.

Placing, moving and removing are covered on [Placing and moving](placing-and-moving/).

## Looks

Some buildings come in several looks. A look is picked from a second dropdown beside the Build tool's circle before the building is placed. The choice is remembered for each building.

- A look is kept when the building is moved.
- The look of a placed building cannot be changed. Destroy it and build it again in another look.
- The Silo's Tower and Shed look is 3×6 tiles. The Tractor facing Front or Back is 1×2 tiles.

## Tap a building

- Tapping a building opens its menu, whatever tool is selected.
- The menu shows what the building does and any controls that belong to it.
- Building menus have no Move or Destroy buttons. Hold a finger on a building to move or destroy it.

Two taps do something else:

- With the Bucket in hand, a tap on a workshop that has finished takes what it made.
- With the Scythe in hand, a tap on a Tractor that has a load unloads it.

The House opens the [Stock Market](../stock-market/) instead of a menu.
