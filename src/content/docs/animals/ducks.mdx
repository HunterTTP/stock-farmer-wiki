Ducks live on water. They have no building. A big enough pond gets ducklings on its own, and each duck can be sold.

<Infobox kind="livestock" id="duck" />

## How ducks arrive

- A pond needs at least one tile with water on all eight sides around it. A pond that is too narrow gets no ducks.
- A pond with room gets its first duckling 10 minutes after the water first appears, then another every 30 minutes while there is room.
- A new farm's ponds each start with no ducks, or with some grown ducks.
- A duckling grows up in <Fact path="animals.livestock.duck.growSeconds" as="duration" />, or <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% faster when fed.
- A duck gives nothing. Its only use is selling.

## How many fit

Each pond has room for one duck per 20 open water tiles in its middle, rounded up. The whole farm holds one duck per 24 water tiles, at least 2 and at most 15.

- New ducks appear only in the middle of a pond (water on all sides), with no other duck within two tiles.
- No 5×5 square of tiles ever holds more than two new ducks. The same limits apply to fish.
- When a pond has no room, it tries again after 30 minutes.
- Ducks already swimming are never moved or removed for crowding.
- Filling in water until no middle is left stops new ducklings.

## Swimming

- Ducks stay on water and never step onto land. They swim anywhere joined water goes, with no distance limit.
- A duck whose water is filled in or built over moves to the nearest water.
- Ducks stay out in the night and the rain.

## Selling

- Tap a duck or duckling and tap **Sell** to sell it for <Fact path="animals.livestock.duck.salePrice" as="money" /> at any age. The sale counts in the day's earnings and frees room for the pond's next duckling.
- Ducks and ducklings are the only animals that can be sold. See [Animal Menu](../animal-menu/).
