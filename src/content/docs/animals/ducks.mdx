Ducks live on water and have no building. A big enough pond gets ducklings on its own, and each duck can be sold.

<Infobox kind="livestock" id="duck" />

## Arrival

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| A pond qualifies | It has at least one tile with water on all eight sides (an interior tile) |
| First duckling | 10 min after the pond first qualifies |
| Next duckling | Every 30 min while the pond and the farm have room |
| No room | It tries again 30 min later |
| Duckling grows up in | <Fact path="animals.livestock.duck.growSeconds" as="duration" /> (<Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% faster while Well Fed) |
| Color | One of four, each equally likely |

- A duck gives nothing. Its only use is selling.
- Ducklings arrive while the game is closed too, caught up for at most three days.

## Room

| Limit | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Per pond | One duck per 20 interior tiles, rounded up |
| Whole farm | One duck per 24 water tiles, at least 2 and at most 15 |

Fish use the same two limits, counted apart from the ducks (see [Fishing](../../gathering/fishing/#where-fish-live)).

### Where a duckling appears

A new duckling picks a random tile that meets all of these:

- an interior tile of its pond, with no decoration on it;
- no other duck within 2 tiles, in any direction;
- no fish on the tile or next to it;
- no 5×5 square around it would then hold more than two ducks.

With no such tile, it waits 30 min. Ducks already swimming are never moved or removed for crowding.

## Swimming

- Ducks stay on water and never step onto land. They swim anywhere joined water goes.
- A duck whose water is filled in or built over moves to the nearest water.
- Ducks stay out at night and in the rain, and never eat crops.
- Filling in water until a pond has no interior tile stops its ducklings.

## A new farm's ducks

Each pond on a new farm starts with a random number of grown ducks, from none up to as many as it has room for, within the farm's limit.

## Selling

A duck or duckling sells for <Fact path="animals.livestock.duck.salePrice" as="money" /> at any age. The sale counts in the day's earnings and frees room for the pond's next duckling. Ducks are the only animals that can be sold.
