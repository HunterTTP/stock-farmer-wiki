Animals have no hunger. They give eggs, milk, wool and fertilizer at their usual pace whether or not they have eaten, and nothing shows over their heads. Feeding by hand is optional and gives a short boost.

## How to feed

- Tap an animal, tap **Feed** and pick a food from the list. The list shows every food in storage with how many you have, and the animal's favorite comes first with a heart.
- Or pick a food in Storage or the Decorate tool's list and drag it onto an animal. This works for every animal, including pets and grown ducks.
- Each feeding uses one of the food.
- An animal that is already Well Fed cannot be fed again until its meal ends.
- A fish fed to an animal is the one the Decorate tool holds, or the lightest of its kind.

## What counts as food

| Food | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Crops | Every crop and every fruit tree basket, rare ones too |
| Fish | Every fish |
| Animal products | Eggs, Milk, Goat Milk |
| Made from food | Flour, Bread, Cheese, and anything made from food such as juice, preserves, pie, soup and pickles |

Animals do not eat wool, fertilizer, logs, stones, ore or what is made from them (cloth, planks, bricks, bars, cut gems, furniture), live chickens, butterflies, fireflies, decorations or fences.

## Well Fed

Every meal gives the same boost for <Fact path="animals.meal.seconds" as="duration" />, whatever the food. A meal fed by hand and a crop an animal eats on its own (see below) give the same boost. A meal cannot be stacked: the next one starts only after it ends.

| Animal | While Well Fed |
| --- | --- |
| Hen | Lays eggs <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% more often |
| Grown farm animal | Makes milk, goat milk, wool or fertilizer <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% faster |
| Baby, duckling or chick | Grows up <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% faster |
| Cat or dog | Is <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% more likely to ripen the crop it stands on |
| Rooster, grown duck | Nothing changes |

- An animal holds one item at a time. A meal speeds it up only until its item is ready. Once ready, the item waits to be collected, fed or not, and the next one starts after you collect it.
- The animal menu shows the meal's time left and turns the status bar purple. See [Animal Menu](../animal-menu/).

- A favorite gives the same boost as any other food.
- Feeding quests ask you to hand-feed favorite foods to animals from a building, and only an animal's favorite food counts. Other foods fed by hand count for nothing. See [Quests](../../quests/).

## Eating crops in the field

Grown chickens and farm animals sometimes eat a crop they walk onto. The crop feeds the animal like a meal by hand.

- The animal is Well Fed for <Fact path="animals.meal.seconds" as="duration" />, with the Feed countdown and the boosted bar in its menu.
- An animal that is already Well Fed is full and leaves crops alone.
- The crop earns no money or XP, and an eaten crop counts toward no feeding quest.
- The rules for which animals eat and how often are on [Animals](../#pests) and [Growth](../../farming/growth/#animals-eating-crops).
