Animals have no hunger. They give eggs, milk, wool and fertilizer at their usual pace whether or not they have eaten. Feeding by hand is optional and gives a short boost called Well Fed.

## Feeding by hand

- Any animal can be fed any food from Storage, including pets and grown ducks. Each feeding uses one of the food.
- A fish fed to an animal is the one you hold, or else the lightest of its kind.
- An animal that is Well Fed cannot be fed again until its meal ends. Meals never stack.

## What counts as food

| Food | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Crops | Every crop and every fruit basket |
| Fish | Every fish |
| Animal products | Eggs, Milk, Goat Milk |
| Made from food | Flour, Bread, Cheese, and goods made from food, such as juice, preserves, pie, soup, pickles and aged cheese |

Wool, fertilizer, logs, stones, ore and what is made from them (cloth, planks, furniture, bricks, bars, polished gems) are not food, and neither are butterflies, fireflies, decorations or fences.

## Well Fed

Every meal gives the same boost for <Fact path="animals.meal.seconds" as="duration" />, whatever the food.

| Animal | While Well Fed |
| --- | --- |
| Hen | Lays <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% more often |
| Grown farm animal | Makes its milk, goat milk, wool or fertilizer <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% faster |
| Baby, duckling or chick | Grows up <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% faster |
| Cat or dog | Is <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% more likely to ripen the crop it stands on |
| Rooster, grown duck | Nothing changes |

How it works: each second of the meal counts as 1.25 seconds of waiting. A wait spent wholly Well Fed takes 0.8 of its usual time, so one meal saves at most 1 min 15 s.

- A meal speeds an item only until it is ready. Once ready it waits to be collected, and no time is saved toward the next one.
- A baby that grows up during its meal gets the boost only for the part of the meal before it grew up, then for its first item.

- A favorite gives the same boost as any other food.
- Feeding quests count only favorite foods fed by hand to animals from the home the quest names. See [Quests](../../quests/).

## Eating crops in the field

Grown chickens and farm animals sometimes eat a crop they stand on (odds on [Growth](../../farming/growth/#animals-eating-crops)). The crop feeds the animal like a meal by hand.

- The animal is Well Fed for <Fact path="animals.meal.seconds" as="duration" />.
- An animal that is already Well Fed leaves crops alone.
- The crop earns no gold or XP and counts toward no quest.
