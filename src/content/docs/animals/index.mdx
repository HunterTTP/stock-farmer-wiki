The farm has pets, chickens, six kinds of farm animal and ducks. Each lives in a home you build, except ducks, which come to ponds on their own. Animals walk around the farm, give things you collect and grow from babies into adults.

## Caring for animals

- Tap an animal to open its [menu](animal-menu/): name, status, Feed, Pet, Nudge and Release or Sell.
- [Feed](feeding/) any animal any food by hand for a short boost. Animals never go hungry and give their items without eating.
- Collect eggs, milk, goat milk, wool and fertilizer on the farm with the Harvest tool. The Bucket takes eggs, milk, goat milk and fertilizer, and the Scythe takes wool (see [Tools](../tools/)).
- Every animal starts as a baby (a chick, kit, duckling, lamb, kid, piglet, foal or calf) and grows up on its own. Animals keep growing and making things while the game is closed.

## Moving around

- Animals wander on grass and farmland. They never walk onto water, buildings, decorations or fences.
- There is no limit on how far from its home an animal walks. Fences, water and buildings are what hold animals in.
- Animals share tiles with each other. Chickens prefer tiles with no other chicken on them.
- Farm animals and chickens keep away from a driving Tractor. Pets do not.
- Ducks stay on water and swim anywhere the water joins up.
- Reopening the game puts every animal back on the tile it was last standing on.

## Night and rain

- At night and in heavy rain, animals with a home walk back to the tile in front of it, step in through the door and disappear from view.
- When the night or the rain is over they come back out and wander off.
- Pond ducks have no home and stay out.
- An animal indoors cannot be tapped and does nothing for the farm: hens lay no eggs, nothing can be milked or shorn, and pets do not ripen crops.

## Pests

- Grown chickens, roosters and grown farm animals out on the land sometimes eat a crop they stand on, growing or ripe.
- Each animal eats at most one crop a day (<Fact path="world.realSecondsPerFarmDay" as="duration" />, one farm day).
- The tile is cleared and earns no money or XP. The animal gets a meal from it, just as if you had fed it by hand (see [Feeding](feeding/#eating-crops-in-the-field)).
- An animal that is already Well Fed leaves crops alone.
- Fruit trees are safe.
- Pets, ducks, babies and chicks leave crops alone.
- An animal eats only the tile it stands on. A fence around a field keeps animals out.
