The farm has pets, chickens, six kinds of farm animal and ducks. Each lives in a home you build, except ducks, which come to ponds on their own. Every animal starts as a baby (a chick, kit, duckling, lamb, kid, piglet, foal or calf) and grows up by itself.

## Time

- Animals never go hungry. They give their items at the same pace whether or not they have eaten. A meal only gives a short boost (see [Feeding](feeding/)).
- Babies grow up, homes have babies and grown animals make their items on the real clock, also while the game is closed. Time away is caught up for at most three days.
- Hens lay eggs, pets ripen crops and animals eat crops only while the game is open.

## Animal menu

Tapping an animal opens its menu. A baby or grown animal that makes something has one status line with a countdown, and a bar that fills as it nears its goal.

| Animal | Status line |
| --- | --- |
| Baby | Grows up in a time |
| Cow, goat, sheep, rabbit, pig, donkey | Next item in a time, with a bar |
| Hen | Next egg in about a time, with no bar (laying is random, and the time is shorter while Well Fed) |
| Pet, rooster, grown duck | None |

## Moving around

- Animals wander on grass and farmland. They never walk onto water, buildings, decorations, rocks, trees or fences.
- There is no limit on how far from its home an animal walks. Fences, water and buildings are what hold animals in.
- Animals can share a tile. Chickens prefer tiles with no other chicken on them.
- Farm animals and chickens keep away from a driving Tractor. Pets do not.
- Ducks stay on water and swim anywhere the water joins up.
- Reopening the game puts every animal back on the tile it was last on.

## Night and rain

- Animals with a home go in once it is half dark (from 8:30 PM) or once the rain is half built up (10 farm minutes after it starts at 2 PM). They come out when the night or the rain is over.
- They walk back to the tile in front of their home, then disappear inside.
- An animal indoors cannot be tapped and does nothing for the farm: hens lay no eggs, nothing can be milked or shorn, pets ripen nothing and nothing eats crops.
- Ducks have no home and stay out.
- In Winter, snow falls all day, but animals only shelter for the night and the rain hours. See [Weather](../world/weather/).

## Pests

Grown chickens and farm animals out on the land sometimes eat the crop they stand on, at most once every <Fact path="world.realSecondsPerFarmDay" as="duration" /> each. The odds are on [Growth](../farming/growth/#animals-eating-crops).

## Names

- Any animal or fish can be given a name of up to 20 characters. A blank name gives back its kind's plain name.
- A name changes nothing about how the animal behaves.

## Releasing and selling

| Animal | What you can do | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duck or duckling | Sell for <Fact path="animals.livestock.duck.salePrice" as="money" /> at any age | The gold counts as a sale in the day's earnings. The pond grows a new duckling in its own time. |
| Chicken or farm animal | Release | It leaves for good, for no gold or XP. Its home has a replacement baby in its own time. |
| Cat or dog | Release | It leaves for good, for no gold or refund. Its Pet House stays empty until you choose a new pet. |

Ducks are the only animals that can be sold.
