---
title: Platforms
description: Where Stock Farmer runs and what differs between iPhone and iPad and Android.
---
Stock Farmer runs on iPhone and iPad, and on Android. Both have the same farm and the same rules.

| Area | iPhone and iPad | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud saves | iCloud, on iOS 26 and later | Google Play Games Saved Games, plus the Android system's own backup |
| Updates | TestFlight or the App Store | Google Play in-app updates, for builds from Google Play |
| "Crops are ready" reminder | Asks for permission to send notifications | Asks once on Android 13 and later |
| Cave | Set down crew to defend a chest, with a daily chest of prizes and crew upgrade cards | Swipe enemies away to defend the Crystal Heart, with ore for a first clear |

- Each farm saves on the device as you play, and syncs to the cloud where available. Up to 10 farms can be kept.
- The game is in a public beta on both (see the [home page](../)).
