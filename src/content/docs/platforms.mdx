title: Platforms
description: Where Stock Farmer runs and what differs between iPhone and iPad and Android.
---
Stock Farmer runs on iPhone and iPad, and on Android.

## iPhone and iPad

- The game supports iPhone and iPad, in portrait, full screen.
- On an iPad everything that floats over the farm is drawn larger: the tool circle, the ring of tools, the dropdowns and the lists they open, which are also wider.
- Messages and banners appear in the top third of an iPhone screen and in the middle of an iPad screen.
- Choose a Version shows the two copies of a farm side by side on an iPad and stacked on an iPhone.
- Farms sync through iCloud on iOS 26 and later. See [Saves and iCloud](../settings/saves-and-icloud/).
- The game is in a public TestFlight beta.

## Android

Android has the same farm, rules and menus, with these differences.

| Area | iPhone and iPad | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud saves | iCloud on iOS 26 and later | Google Play Games Saved Games. The Android system's own backup is on as well. |
| Update prompt | TestFlight or the App Store | Google Play in-app updates, for builds from Google Play |
| Reminder permission | Asked for the "crops are ready" reminder | Asked once on Android 13 and later, while the game opens |
