---
title: Settings
description: The few settings that touch how the game plays, and what they leave alone.
---

No setting changes prices, timers, odds or any other rule. Most settings change only sound, looks or controls. These are the ones that touch play:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Gameplay → Tutorial | Shows or hides this farm's tutorial and tips. It is the one setting kept per farm. While a tutorial step needs it, a Common fish is put in a pond and small trees, rocks or a Copper Vein are put near you. |
| Graphics → Weather effects | Off hides rain, snow, falling leaves and petals. The weather still happens: animals still shelter and butterflies still stay away. |
| Graphics → Clouds | Off hides the clouds and their shadows. Clouds change nothing in the game. |

Every farm has its own save. Farms save by themselves as you play.
