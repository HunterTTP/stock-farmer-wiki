A new farm is a <Fact path="world.mapSize" /> by <Fact path="world.mapSize" /> tile map with ponds, a forest, rocks and ore veins already on it, and a few tiles of farmland in the middle.

## Starting a farm

1. Open the Load Game menu and tap **New Farm**. With no farms yet, the New Game form opens on its own.
2. Type a **Farm name**. A name must be different from every other farm's and up to 24 characters.
3. Optionally open **Advanced Options** to see or type a World Seed.
4. Tap **Create**.

- The Load Game menu lists every farm, most recently played first.
- Each farm's "..." menu ends with **Properties**, a read-only page with the farm's name and its seed (which you can copy).

## What a new farm starts with

| | Starts with |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 8 AM on day 1, in Summer |
| Money | $0 |
| Farmland | <Fact path="world.startingFarmland" /> tiles in the middle of the map |
| Crops | Wheat, whose seeds are free |
| Scythe and Bucket | Set to Sell. What you gather sells at once. |
| Ponds | 2 or 3, which may start with ducks and fish. At least one fish is rare. |
| Forest | Trees of every kind and size, thin near the middle and thick toward the edge of the map |
| Rocks | Spread evenly over the map |
| Ore veins | Copper first, then Copper and Silver, with enough copper and gems to build everything once |

- Three ponds are a small, a medium and a large one anywhere on the map. Two ponds are a large one and a medium or a second large one, in opposite corners.
- Ponds keep 5 tiles of grass between each other, stay out of the middle of the map and keep 3 tiles of grass from the map's edge.
- No tree stands within 3 tiles of the starting farmland or on water.
- The first two veins on every map are Copper.

## World Seed

A world seed describes a whole new map. The same seed always makes the same map on any device running the same version of the game.

- A seed looks like `7K3M-Q9TX-B111`.
- A random seed fills the field each time the form opens. Tap **Copy** to copy it and share it.
- Type or paste a seed to use another map. Capitals, lower case, spaces and dashes all work. An O reads as a zero and an I or L as a one.
- A seed that does not read shows a message under the field, and **Create** waits.

## Tutorial

A tutorial starts on its own the first time a new farm loads. Settings → Gameplay → Tutorial turns it off for a farm.
