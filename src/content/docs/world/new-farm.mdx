A new farm is a <Fact path="world.mapSize" /> by <Fact path="world.mapSize" /> tile map with ponds, a forest, rocks and ore veins already on it, and a few tiles of farmland in the middle.

## What a new farm starts with

| | Starts with |
| --- | --- |
| Time | 8 AM on day 1, in the Starting Season you pick (Spring, Summer, Fall or Winter), Summer unless you change it |
| Money | $0 |
| Level | 1, with 0 XP |
| Farmland | <Fact path="world.startingFarmland" /> tiles in the middle of the map |
| Crops | Wheat, whose seeds are free |
| Scythe and Bucket | Set to Sell |
| Ponds | 2 or 3 |
| Ducks and fish | Each pond starts with between none and as many ducks as it has room for, and each fish place has a fish half the time. At least one fish is Rare. |
| Forest | Pines and Oaks of every size, thin near the middle and thick toward the edge |
| Rocks and veins | <Fact path="mining.rocksOnANewMap.min" /> to <Fact path="mining.rocksOnANewMap.max" /> rocks spread evenly, <Fact path="mining.veinsOnANewMap.min" /> to <Fact path="mining.veinsOnANewMap.max" /> of them veins (see [Mining](../../gathering/mining/#a-new-farms-rocks-and-veins)) |

## Ponds

- Half of all maps have three ponds: a small, a medium and a large one, anywhere on the map.
- The other half have two, in opposite corners: a large one and, as often as not, a medium or a second large one.
- Ponds keep 5 tiles of grass between each other and 3 from the map's edge, and stay at least 8 tiles from the starting farmland.

## Forest

- No tree stands within 3 tiles of the starting farmland or on water.
- The chance of a tree rises from nothing at the middle of the map to certain at its edge.

## World Seed

A world seed describes a whole new map. The same seed always makes the same map on any device running the same version of the game.

- A seed looks like `7K3M-Q9TX-B111`. A random one is offered for each new farm, and it can be copied and shared.
- Typed seeds ignore capitals, spaces and dashes. An O reads as a zero and an I or L as a one.
- A farm's seed can be looked up in its properties later.
