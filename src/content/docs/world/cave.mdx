The Cave is tower defence in a room of rock. Enemies come out of the doors and follow the paths to a chest in the middle. You set down your Shroom Crew before Start, and they fight on their own. There are <Fact path="cave.levels" /> playable levels across 10 biomes of <Fact path="cave.room.zoneLevels" /> levels each, with a guaranteed first-clear reward on every level and two rewards on every boss level.

Pinch to zoom in on the room and back out to see all of it, and drag to pan around the room just like on the farm.

## Controls

- The Cave has no tool bar. You pick crew from the crew list down the left, which reads "Recruited" with how many crew you have set down out of the level's crew slots.
- The crew list shows up to five and a half crew. With more, swipe it up or down to see the rest.
- Drag a crew sideways off the list to set it down. It leaves the list at once. If it can't stand where you let go, it goes back to its place.
- Drag a placed crew back onto the list to take it off.
- Start sits at the bottom centre.
- The wave bar at the top shows the biome and level position (such as Earth · 4/10 or Earth · Boss), with the red Exit door at its left end and the purple Levels button at its right end.
- In setup, Levels opens the 10-level biome section grid, and tapping the wave bar between Exit and Levels opens the detail card for the level you are on: its first-clear reward (or two boss rewards), its enemies and Play (or free Replay on a beaten level). Tapping Play, Replay or the x closes it with your crew as they were.
- In a fight or after a win, Levels is greyed out and the bar between Exit and Levels takes no taps.
- In a fight, Exit pauses it (Resume, Restart or Exit to Farm). Restarting returns your Shroom Crew to the sidebar so you can place them fresh. In setup or after a win, Exit leaves the Cave at once.

## Biomes

Every <Fact path="cave.room.zoneLevels" /> levels the Cave changes to a new biome, with its own look, liquid, enemies and boss. Each biome ends on its boss level.

<DataTable name="caveZones" />

- A biome's liquid pours from a fall in the back wall and runs as a river to another wall. Some rivers widen into one big pool, and a few rooms have big pools instead.
- Paths cross the liquid on small bridges, never more than <Fact path="cave.room.longestBridge" /> tiles long.
- No crew stand on liquid or on a bridge.
- Crystal and standing rock fill some floor tiles in the Ice, Amethyst, Radioactive, Lava and Prism biomes. No crew stand on them. Prism's crystals come in ruby, sapphire and diamond.
- Prism has rainbow water, glitter and rainbow glints. Circuit has a data stream, circuit traces on the floor under a pulsing grid, and data bits that rise. Space has void, a starry nebula floor with twinkling stars, spirals, shooting stars, and rocks that float over their shadows.
- The lanterns in the corners match the biome. Prism's are cool white, Circuit's cyan and Space's star blue.
- Small bats, ants, inchworms, spiders and other cave life wander the room. They are only scenery: they never go on the paths and can't be hit.

## The room and its doors

Every level has its own room and paths. The layout of each level is fixed and the same in every game.

- The room grows with the biomes, from 11×11 tiles up to <Fact path="cave.room.largestRoom" />×<Fact path="cave.room.largestRoom" />. A ring of rock closes it in, and the chest stands in the middle.
- A door is a dark mouth in the wall. Every enemy comes out of a door and follows its path to the chest. The biomes table gives each biome's doors.
- Every path is in plain view from its door to the chest.
- Paths never cross. Paths that meet share the rest of the way to the chest.
- From Level <Fact path="cave.room.threePathsFrom" /> every room has at least 3 doors, and the paths meet in at least <Fact path="cave.room.fewestJoins" /> places before the chest, so one crew member can guard two paths where they join.
- From Level <Fact path="cave.room.threePathsFrom" /> each door's path runs on its own for its first tiles (the biomes' rooms keep 2 or 3) before it joins another, so two doors never look like one path.
- Deeper levels have longer, windier paths.
- Every room keeps enough open floor beside the paths for the level's crew.

## Enemies

The Cave's creatures are its enemies. Every small enemy falls to one hit. Golems and bosses take many.

- Every enemy stays on its door's path. Walkers walk it, flyers fly along it and Moles leap along it.
- Each biome leads with a new enemy. A level sends two to four kinds of small enemy: the biome's new one and a few earlier ones that suit it.
- Golems come only in the Ice, Lava and Space biomes, and as bosses. No more than 3 golems are out at once.

### Earth

The first biome is Earth (Levels 1 to 10). Its early levels introduce one enemy at a time before ramping up to the first boss.

| Level | What comes | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pebbles only | Set down the crew. The Sword hits and the Shield holds a path. |
| 2 | Moles with a few Pebbles | Moles dig under Shields. Hit them when they pop up. |
| 3 | Bats with a few Pebbles | Bats fly over Shields. The Bow reaches them. |
| 4 | All three together | Place crew where paths join. |
| 5 to 9 | Larger waves of Moles, Bats and Pebbles | Build speed and positioning before the boss. |
| 10 | Two waves of all three, then the Stone Boss | The first boss. |

The Water biome (Levels 11 to 20) sends Moles with Pebbles beside them, ending with the Mole Chief on Level 20.

### Small enemies

Speed is in tiles a second on the easiest levels.

<DataTable name="caveEnemies" />

- A Mole leaps along its path and dips underground between leaps. It can't be hit underground, and it leaps past Shields. In a Lantern's light it walks like any walker, so a Shield holds it and the crew can hit it.
- A Glow Bug's shell: every hit bounces off it, and the Bow and the Sniper never aim at it, until acid melts the shell (see [Shells and acid](#shells-and-acid)).
- A Geode hides among the crystals. The crew only aim at it in a Lantern's light. A Shield still holds it, and a bomb or acid still hits it in the dark. Away from the light it fades into the crystals.
- A Glare Slime flashes every few seconds. Crew right beside it are dazzled and stop for a few seconds. A Mender's ring keeps them clear, and a frozen Glare Slime doesn't flash.
- A Glitch Newt blinks a few tiles ahead every few seconds and steals <Fact path="cave.glitchNewtTheft" /> hearts instead of <Fact path="cave.chestTheft" />. A Shield stops it. See [Thieves](#thieves).
- Ember Bats are Bats that come in big flocks.
- A split puts the two Mini Slimes on the spot, as many as the room has places for.
- Small enemies move faster on deeper levels, as a multiple of the speeds above: 1 on Levels 1 and 2, 1.3 on Level 5, 1.45 on Level 10, 1.6 on Level 25, 1.75 on Level 50 and 1.9 on Level 100, rising evenly between. Golems and bosses keep their own pace.

### New enemies

- A level's detail card marks an enemy you have not met yet as NEW.
- After Play, a card in the room names the new enemy and says in one line how it behaves and what stops it. Tap it to close it and set down your crew.
- Each enemy gets its card once.

### Lean levels and their doors

Most levels lean to one of their biome's kinds of small enemy, which comes in bigger numbers. The level list labels a level by its lean: Swarm (Slimes), Rush (Glitch Newts), Wing (Bats), Burrow (Moles) or Stone (golems and Glow Bugs). A biome's tough level and its boss level are Mixed.

- On a lean level the lean kind comes out of one door only, two from Level <Fact path="cave.room.twoLeanDoorsFrom" /> in a room with 3 or more doors.
- A level that sends just a few Bats sends them out of one door.
- Every other kind comes out of every door.

### Golems

Golems come in three sizes: small, medium and large. Deeper levels send more and bigger golems, and they take more hits to beat.

- Ice Golems (Ice biome) and Void Golems (Space) are golems in the biome's colours.
- A Void Golem slowly heals unless it is burning.
- Whatever the path's length, a golem reaches the chest the time below after it steps out.

## Waves

Enemies come in waves. The wave bar at the top shows the biome and level, the wave you are on and the time to the next one. The tables give each level's waves.

- A new wave starts 6 seconds after the last enemy of the one before has come out, whether or not its enemies are beaten.
- While the next wave waits, its small enemies trickle in one at a time: the first after 1 second, then one every 2.5 seconds.
- Golems never trickle in. They come with their own wave.
- A boss gives warning before it steps out: the paths turn red from its door, the room shakes harder and a low rumble ends in a thud. The paths stay red until the boss is beaten, then fade back.
- Each step a walking boss takes lands with a soft thud, a puff of dust and a tiny shake of the room (no shake with Reduce Motion).
- The level is won once every wave has come and every enemy is beaten or has run off.

## The chest

- The chest is the orange Trunk on standard levels, and the gunmetal grey Trunk on boss levels.
- The chest has <Fact path="cave.chestHealth" /> health on every level.
- A small enemy that reaches the chest steals <Fact path="cave.chestTheft" /> health, once, then turns and runs back out along its path. Your crew can still beat it on the way out. Once it is out of its door it is gone.
- A golem or a boss that reaches the chest stays and takes <Fact path="cave.chestDrainPerSecond" /> health a second until it is beaten, whatever its size. The Glitch Boss is the exception: it grabs and runs (see [Thieves](#thieves)).
- Stolen hearts stay lost even if your crew beats the thief on its way out.
- At 0 health the level is lost. A loss pays nothing and does not count as cleared. Retries and replays are always free (see [First-clear rewards and replays](#first-clear-rewards-and-replays)).

## Boss levels and walls

Every tenth level (10, 20 and so on through <Fact path="cave.levels" />) is a boss level and a wall, marked as Hard on the level list. The boss comes in the last wave with a few small enemies of its own kind. The waves before it send the level's usual mix. Each boss is a giant of a small enemy you already know, with that enemy's one trick. Level <Fact path="cave.levels" /> ends on the Moss Boss.

<DataTable name="caveBosses" />

- Until Start, the door the boss will come out of shows the boss's small picture. On a replay the boss can come out of another door, and the picture is always at the one it uses.
- When a boss enters, a lower-third intro card shows its picture, biome and level, name and a one-line tip (tap to dismiss, or it clears on its own after a couple of seconds).
- Every boss except the Glitch Boss stays at the chest and pounds it until it is beaten. The Great Bat lands on the chest, where the Sword can reach it.
- A boss's health is its share of the level's boss hits, shown in the table below.
- Once the boss is beaten, the small enemies still to come stay away.
- A Sword barely moves a boss.
- The first clear of a boss level grants two rewards: a new crew kind (Levels 10 to 90) and an 8-card Random Skin roulette, or <Fact path="cave.rewards.roughDiamond" /> Rough Diamond and a Random Skin roulette on Level <Fact path="cave.levels" /> (see [Boss rewards](#boss-rewards)).

<DataTable name="caveBossLevels" />

Unbeaten non-boss levels in each 10-level biome show 1 to 4 flames as the section climbs (1 on positions 1 and 2, 2 on 3 and 4, 3 on 5 to 7, and 4 on 8 and 9). Every tenth level shows a boss crown.

## Shroom Crew

Your crew are Shrooms set down on the floor before Start. They are the only way to fight: they hit the enemies on their own. A crew member's hit is one plain hit.

- Crew are free to set down. A level takes one of each kind unlocked, up to its crew slots below.
- Every crew member the level takes must be set down before Start.
- A Shield stands on a path tile, never in a doorway or on a bridge. Every other kind stands on open floor beside the paths. No crew stand on rocks, crystal, liquid, the chest or each other.
- A Mender can stand anywhere on the floor.
- Crew can be moved or taken back until Start.
- Crew never level up. Crew upgrade cards make a kind stronger for good (see [Crew upgrade cards](#crew-upgrade-cards)).
- Crew skins change only how a crew looks (see [Skins](../../buildings/skins/)).

<DataTable name="caveCrew" />

- Sword: hits the nearest ground enemy in reach, never a flyer, and pushes it back.
- Shield: holds walkers on its path until its shield breaks.
- Bow: shoots an enemy in range, flyers first. It never aims at an enemy with a live shell.
- Frost: freezes every enemy near it on each pulse. It does no damage.
- Bomber: throws a bomb at the biggest crowd of ground enemies in range (best against Slimes and ground crowds). The bomb lands where the crowd was and goes off a moment later. The blast hits every ground enemy in its spread, skips flying Bats, and bounces off unmelted Glow Bugs.
- Sniper: hits one enemy anywhere in the room, a golem or boss first, and of those the one nearest the chest. It never misses, and never aims at an enemy with a live shell.
- Lantern: Moles in its light can't dig, so they walk, and a Geode in its light can be aimed at.
- Alchemist: splashes acid on a shelled enemy, hitting ground enemies in the splash and skipping flyers. It doesn't speed up other crew.
- Puffball: puffs spores that hit every flyer near it.
- Mender: crew in its ring can't be dazzled. It doesn't heal the chest.
- Trapper: sets snares on the path that hold walkers.
- Torch: sets an enemy alight, and a burning enemy can't heal.

Each crew type is for one job. The sections below say how its power works.

### Crew powers

- **Frost** freezes enemies for a moment. Golems and bosses stay frozen half as long. A frozen enemy stands still, and it can't chew a Shield, pound the chest or steal. Frost freezes flyers and Moles above ground, and a Mole underground is out of its reach. Cards add freeze time and reach.
- **Puffball** puffs spores every few seconds. They hit every flyer near it and leave ground enemies alone. Cards widen the cloud and speed up the spores.
- **Trapper** sets snares on the path near it, and never more than 2 out at once. A snare holds the first walker that steps on it, coming in or running off with a theft, and then goes. Golems and bosses are held half as long. Flyers and Moles that are digging pass over. Cards hold snares longer and set them sooner.
- **Torch** sets the enemy nearest the chest alight every few seconds. A burning enemy keeps losing health for a few seconds and can't heal. Cards burn longer and reach further.
- **Mender** has a green ring on the floor. Crew inside it are never dazzled.

### Shells and acid

- A Glow Bug and the Pill Bug Queen wear a shell. Every hit bounces off it, the Torch's fire too, and the Bow and the Sniper don't aim at them. A bounce shows as "bounce".
- The Alchemist splashes acid every few seconds on the shelled enemy nearest the chest. It melts the shell for good and gives every ground enemy in the splash a small hit, skipping flyers. Cards make it splash sooner.
- Once a shell is melted, anything can hurt that enemy, and the Bow and the Sniper aim at it. Acid also hits a Geode in the dark.

### Thieves

- A thief is a small enemy that steals from the chest and runs back out. Your crew can still hit it on its way out, and a snare can catch it.
- Stolen hearts never come back, even if your crew beats the thief on its way out.
- The Glitch Newt steals <Fact path="cave.glitchNewtTheft" /> hearts. The Glitch Boss steals <Fact path="cave.glitchBossHeartGrabPercent" />% of the chest's hearts and runs. If it gets out the level goes on, with those hearts lost.

### Tutorial

The Cave tutorial runs across Levels 1 to 4 and the Level 10 boss in a reserved top guide slot, using short story callouts, hands-on setup steps and brief time-paused spotlights during fights.

- Level 1 introduces the chest and its hearts, highlights the door and path enemies follow, guides dragging Sword, Shield and Bow into place with live range rings and lit covered path tiles, shows the crew stat card (`>`), and pauses briefly when an enemy first enters range, hits a Shield or steals a chest heart.
- Levels 2 to 4 and Level 10 each teach one new mechanic: two doors and Moles digging under Shields on Level 2, Bow range coverage and Bats flying over Shields on Level 3, joining paths on Level 4 and the ringed boss door on Level 10.
- Dragging any Shroom Crew member highlights the path tiles covered inside its range ring in both the tutorial and normal play.
- Tapping Exit Tutorial turns off the Cave tutorial (and resumes time if paused on a spotlight) without changing the farm tutorial.
- Settings → Gameplay → Replay Cave Tutorial replays the Cave tutorial from Earth Level 1 on your next visit.
- The first time you have a Lantern, and the first time a level takes a fourth crew member, a short hint says what it is for.

### Misses

The Sword, the Bow and the Bomber sometimes miss. The table's Hits row is the share of swings, shots or blasts that land.

- A miss still uses up the swing, shot or blast. A tiny "miss" shows beside the enemy.
- A Bomber's miss is a dud: the bomb lands and fizzles, and does nothing.
- The Sniper never misses. The Alchemist, Puffball and Torch always land. The Shield, Frost, Lantern, Mender and Trapper don't hit, so they never miss.
- Accuracy cards raise the Hits share (see [Crew upgrade cards](#crew-upgrade-cards)).

### The Shield

- A walker on the Shield's path stops at the edge of the Shield's tile and chews its health each second: any small enemy 1, and a golem by its size (see [Golems](#golems)). A boss chews hardest.
- Flyers go straight over a Shield, and Moles leap past under it unless a Lantern lights them.
- A broken Shield lets walkers by and grows back with full health after <Fact path="cave.shield.regrowSeconds" /> seconds.
- A Sword's push moves golems less than small enemies (see [Golems](#golems)).

## Crew upgrade cards

Each 10-level biome awards 2 Shroom Crew upgrade cards as guaranteed first-clear rewards, on its 4th and 9th levels (<Fact path="cave.rewards.upgradeCards" /> cards across all <Fact path="cave.levels" /> levels).

- Each card adds one rank to an upgrade of a crew kind you have unlocked. An upgrade holds up to <Fact path="cave.upgradeMaxRank" /> ranks, and an upgrade at <Fact path="cave.upgradeMaxRank" /> ranks never comes up.
- Each upgrade is named for the stat it raises, such as Knockback, Range, Accuracy, Attack Speed or Attack Power (see the table below).
- If every upgrade of your unlocked crew is already at <Fact path="cave.upgradeMaxRank" /> ranks, 4 Gold Ore is awarded instead.
- Ranks last for good and work on every level.

### New crew kinds

- The first time you beat a boss level from Level 10 to Level 90, you unlock the next crew kind in order alongside an 8-card Random Skin roulette: Frost (Level 10), Bomber (Level 20), Sniper (Level 30), Lantern (Level 40), Alchemist (Level 50), Puffball (Level 60), Mender (Level 70), Trapper (Level 80), then Torch (Level 90).
- Each biome's boss unlocks the crew type that suits the biome after it.
- Replays give no bonus. On Level <Fact path="cave.levels" />, all crew are already unlocked, so Space's boss awards <Fact path="cave.rewards.roughDiamond" /> Rough Diamond alongside a Random Skin roulette.
- Crew kinds unlock only this way. The Sword, the Shield and the Bow are unlocked from the start.

## Winning a level

When the last enemy falls:

1. Your crew jump for joy where they stand (or bob gently with Reduce Motion).
2. On a first clear, the chest lid opens with a glow, and each earned reward pops out one by one in its own unlock card (ore, crew upgrade stat card, new crew card or the 8-card Random Skin roulette). Tap OK inside each card to move on.
3. After your rewards are claimed (or straight away on a replay), the clear summary card shows the biome and level badge, animated star rating, fight stats (time, chest hearts kept and score) and a recap of what you won.
4. Tapping OK returns to the 10-level biome grid, where newly earned stars pop onto the cleared tile and the padlock drops off the next level.

- Rewards are stored with the farm at the win, so leaving early never loses them.
- Won skins apply to your collection at once.
- An ore or gem reward goes into [Storage](../../storage-and-selling/). If it doesn't fit, "Storage is full" appears with Make room to open the Storage page (see [Making room](#making-room)).

### Making room

The Storage page opens from Make room while an ore or gem reward doesn't fit in Storage.

- A red card at the top reads "Make room for 6 Copper Ore", with a slot for each piece. It counts down as the ore goes in and turns green with "All 6 stored".
- Under it are how full Storage is and every stored item, sorted by value or count. Each item has Sell 1 and Sell All with what they pay, or Trash 1 and Trash All for things that can't be sold, as in Storage.
- Each time you make room, a waiting ore flies from its slot into its row, one at a time.
- OK closes the page and returns to the summary. Ore that still doesn't fit keeps waiting until you make room.

Every playable level (1 to <Fact path="cave.levels" />) has a fixed first-clear reward: 1 guaranteed reward on each non-boss level and 2 rewards on each boss level, for <Fact path="cave.rewards.totalRewards" /> total first-clear rewards across all 10 biomes.

### Boss rewards

- Every boss skin roulette picks 8 unowned skins from across the whole game (buildings, Trunk and Shroom Crew): 2 Common, 2 Rare, 2 Epic and 2 Legendary, backfilling from remaining unowned skins if a tier runs low.

### Replays

- Every try on a new level is free, and a loss uses nothing up, so you can retry until you win.
- Replaying any cleared level is always free for 5-star diamond mastery, with no repeat drops.
- Replays shuffle which doors the boss, golems and marked enemies come out of on the same paths with the same enemies. Restart keeps the shuffled doors.

## Biome cooldowns

New levels come in 10-level biomes: Levels 1 to 10, 11 to 20, and so on through Level <Fact path="cave.levels" />. Each biome ends on a boss level.

- Within a biome, each level opens as soon as you clear the one before it.
- After you clear a biome's boss level for the first time (Levels 10 to 90), a biome cooldown holds the next biome for 1 real hour. The hour counts down while the game is closed too. There is no cooldown after Level <Fact path="cave.levels" />.
- Replays and losses don't start or reset the hour.
- You can wait out the hour for free or spend gold via Open Now to open the next biome immediately. The Open Now price decreases by whole minutes remaining (rounded up to the nearest 10 gold) from each biome's full 1-hour price below.

<DataTable name="caveGates" />

- While a biome cooldown is active, entering the Cave shows the next biome room with the live countdown and Open Now card, and future locked biomes stay collapsed in the Levels grid until you reach them.
- Level <Fact path="cave.levels" /> is the deepest playable level.

## Stars

A win is rated from 1 to 5 stars in half steps (5 is a diamond) out of 100 points, and the level keeps its best rating. Replays are rated too. Stars pay nothing.

- Up to 55 points are for the chest's health kept. Full marks need 70% of the health kept on Level 1, and a little more on each deeper level.
- Up to 45 points are for speed: how soon the last enemy is beaten once the last one has come out. Deeper levels ask for a quicker finish.

| 80 | 4.5 |
