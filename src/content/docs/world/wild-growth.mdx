Wild trees, rocks and ore veins grow and appear only during the midnight wave (see [Day and Night](../day-and-night/#midnight)). Each night does two things: wild things may grow a size, and new things appear.

## Growing

Each wild tree, rock and vein that can grow has a 1 in 5 chance each night to grow one size.

| Thing | Grows into |
| --- | --- |
| Small, Medium or Big tree | The next size of the same tree, up to Huge |
| Small or Medium rock | The next size up, up to Big |
| Small Silver Vein | Silver Vein |
| Huge tree, Big rock, every other vein | Nothing |

- A tree grows only if the bigger tree's picture has bare grass to spread over and stays off the four tiles in front of the cave entrance. It keeps its trunk on the same tile.
- A rock stands on one tile at every size, so it always has room.
- Trees you planted and rocks you placed never grow.

## New things

Each night has 7 to 10 draws (4 to 6 for trees, 2 or 3 for rocks and 1 for a vein, added together). Each draw picks a tree, a plain rock or a vein of one ore at once, by the weights below.

| Limit | Trees | Plain rocks | Ore veins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Most new in one night | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| Most wild on the map | 390 | 40 | 12 |

- A draw of a kind that has reached its limit for the night or the map leaves nothing, so a plentiful kind comes less often without handing its draws to the others.
- A new thing appears on a random empty tile of bare grass, away from the starting farmland, from the front of animal homes and from the four tiles in front of the cave entrance, in the map's top-right corner.
- A new tree is a Pine or an Oak of a random size and color. A new rock is Small 50%, Medium 35% or Big 15% of the time. Half of new Silver Veins are small.
- Things you planted or placed never count toward a limit.

## What the farm is short of

Each draw's weight is worked out from the map, your Storage and your level:

> Weight = usual share × scarcity × (1 + shortage)

| Part | Trees | Plain rocks | A vein of one ore |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Usual share | 5 | 2.5 | 1 × the ore's share at your level (see [Mining](../../gathering/mining/#odds-of-each-ore)) |
| Scarcity | 1 − wild trees ÷ 390 | 1 − wild plain rocks ÷ 40 | 1 − veins of that ore ÷ (12 × its share) |
| Shortage | Share of the logs you lack for the building that needs the most logs, among those unlocked up to 2 levels above yours | The same for stones | Copper only: share of 12 Copper Ore you lack |

- Scarcity never drops below 0.1, so a kind the map has plenty of still comes now and then until its limit.
- Shortage runs from 0 with enough in Storage to 1 with none, so a kind you are short of comes up to twice as often.
- When everything is equally scarce and nothing is short, a night leaves about 5 trees, 2.5 rocks and 1 vein.

### Example

A farm with 195 wild trees (half the limit), 40 plain rocks (at the limit) and plenty of logs gives trees a weight of 5 × 0.5 = 2.5 and rocks none, since the map is full of them.

## When it happens

- A new thing or a growth appears when the wave reaches its tile.
- Leaving the game while the wave rolls grows the rest of that night at once.
- Nothing grows for time away. A night happens only when the game is open at midnight.
