Trees, rocks and ore veins appear and grow only while the midnight wave rolls out over the map. See [Day and Night](../day-and-night/) for the wave. Each night does two things: wild things grow a size, and new things appear.

## Growing

Each wild tree, rock and vein has a 1 in 5 chance each night to grow one size, if it has room.

| Thing | Grows into |
| --- | --- |
| Small, Medium or Big tree | The next size of the same tree, up to Huge |
| Small or Medium rock | The next size up, up to Big |
| Small Silver Vein | Silver Vein |
| Huge tree, Big rock, other veins | Nothing. They stay as they are. |

- A tree grows only if the bigger tree has bare grass to spread over. It keeps its trunk on the same tile.
- A rock stands on one tile at every size, so it always has room.
- Trees you plant and rocks you place never grow. Wild means the ones the map was made with or a night left.

## New things

Each night draws up to 7 to 10 new things from three kinds: trees, plain rocks and ore veins. A night leaves at most 8 trees, 4 rocks and 2 veins.

- Each appears on a random empty tile anywhere on the map: bare grass, away from the starting farmland and from building entrances.
- A kind stops coming once the map holds its limit of wild ones. Things you plant or place do not count toward a limit.
- A kind that is already common on the map comes less often, but never stops until its limit.

| Limit on wild things | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Trees | 390 |
| Rocks | 40 |
| Ore veins | 12 |

## What the farm is short of

Each night's draws favor what your farm lacks for your level.

- Trees and rocks come more often when the map has few of them.
- A kind comes more often when your Storage is short of what you build with: the Logs and Stones for the dearest building you can build within two levels, and Copper Ore.
- Copper Veins are the common ore at low levels.
- Silver, Gold, Ruby and Sapphire Veins come less often at low levels. They rise the closer you get to the level you can process them. Smelting and cutting open at level <Fact path="quests.oreQuestsFromLevel" />.
- Once you can process every ore, the ores come in their usual mix.

## When it happens

- A new thing or a growth appears when the wave's front reaches its tile. It fades in over about 1.5 seconds with a soft glow in the wave's color.
- With Reduce Motion on, the wave is not drawn and things simply appear.
- Leaving the game while the wave rolls grows the rest of that night at once.
- Nothing grows for time away. A night happens only when the game is open at midnight.
