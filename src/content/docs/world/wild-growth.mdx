Wild trees, rocks and ore veins grow and appear only during the midnight wave (see [Day and Night](../day-and-night/#midnight)). Each night does two things: wild things may grow a size, and new things appear.

## Growing

Each wild tree, rock and vein that can grow has a 1 in <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.growthOddsDenominator" /> (<Fact path="world.wildGrowth.growthChancePercent" />%) chance each night to grow one size.

| Thing | Grows into |
| --- | --- |
| Small, Medium or Big tree | The next size of the same tree, up to Huge |
| Small or Medium rock | The next size up, up to Big |
| Small Iron Vein | Iron Vein |
| Huge tree, Big rock, every other vein | Nothing |

- A tree grows only if the bigger tree's picture has bare grass to spread over and stays off the <Fact path="world.newFarm.caveMouthClearingTiles" /> tiles in front of the cave entrance. It keeps its trunk on the same tile.
- A rock stands on one tile at every size, so it always has room.
- Trees you planted and rocks you placed never grow.

## New things

Each night has <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyTotal.min" /> to <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyTotal.max" /> draws (<Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyTrees.min" /> to <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyTrees.max" /> for trees, <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyRocks.min" /> or <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyRocks.max" /> for rocks and <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyVeins.min" /> for a vein, added together). Each draw picks a tree, a plain rock or a vein of one ore at once, by the weights below.

| Limit | Trees | Plain rocks | Ore veins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Most new in one night | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyMaxTrees" /> | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyMaxRocks" /> | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.nightlyMaxVeins" /> |
| Most wild on the map | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.treeCap" /> | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.rockCap" /> | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.veinCap" /> |

- A draw of a kind that has reached its limit for the night or the map leaves nothing, so a plentiful kind comes less often without handing its draws to the others.
- A new thing appears on a random empty tile of bare grass, away from the starting farmland, from the front of animal homes and from the <Fact path="world.newFarm.caveMouthClearingTiles" /> tiles in front of the cave entrance, in the map's top-right corner.
- A new tree is a Pine or an Oak of a random size and color. A new rock is Small <Fact path="mining.newMap.smallRockPercent" />%, Medium <Fact path="mining.newMap.mediumRockPercent" />% or Big <Fact path="mining.newMap.bigRockPercent" />% of the time. Half of new Iron Veins are small.
- Things you planted or placed never count toward a limit.

## What the farm is short of

Each draw's weight is worked out from the map, your Storage and your level:

> Weight = usual share × scarcity × (1 + shortage)

| Part | Trees | Plain rocks | A vein of one ore |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Usual share | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.treeShare" /> | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.rockShare" /> | <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.veinShare" /> × the ore's share at your level (see [Mining](../../gathering/mining/#odds-of-each-ore)) |
| Scarcity | 1 − wild trees ÷ <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.treeCap" /> | 1 − wild plain rocks ÷ <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.rockCap" /> | 1 − veins of that ore ÷ (<Fact path="world.wildGrowth.veinCap" /> × its share) |
| Shortage | Share of the logs you lack for the building that needs the most logs, among those unlocked up to <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.buildingLookaheadLevels" /> levels above yours | The same for stones | Share of the ore you lack for the building that needs the most of it (among those unlocked up to <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.buildingLookaheadLevels" /> levels above yours), with at least <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.copperShortageTarget" /> for Copper Ore |

- Scarcity never drops below <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.scarcityFloor" />, so a kind the map has plenty of still comes now and then until its limit.
- Shortage runs from 0 with enough in Storage to <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.shortageBoost" /> with none, so a kind you are short of comes up to twice as often.
- When everything is equally scarce and nothing is short, a night leaves about <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.treeShare" /> trees, <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.rockShare" /> rocks and <Fact path="world.wildGrowth.veinShare" /> vein.

### Example

A farm with 195 wild trees (half the limit), 40 plain rocks (at the limit) and plenty of logs gives trees a weight of 5 × 0.5 = 2.5 and rocks none, since the map is full of them.

## When it happens

- A new thing or a growth appears when the wave reaches its tile.
- Leaving the game while the wave rolls grows the rest of that night at once.
- Nothing grows for time away. A night happens only when the game is open at midnight.
