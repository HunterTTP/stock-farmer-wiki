Trees can be planted with the Decorate tool in any size and color. Wild trees, which grow on their own, are on [Trees, Logs and Stones](../../gathering/trees-logs-and-stones/).

<DataTable name="trees" />

- "Cost to plant" is for one tree of that size. Every tree is available from the start and needs no materials.
- Birch never grows wild, so it only comes from planting.
- A Brown Oak looks green or blue-green in Spring and Summer, and a Brown or Yellow Birch looks green in Summer. Each shows its own color in Fall and Winter.

## Rules

- A tree stands on one tile at every size, and its picture rises over the tiles behind it.
- It goes on bare ground only: not on water, farmland, a fence, a crop, a building, a decoration or another tree, and not in front of an animal home or where it would hide the cave entrance.
- Animals walk past it and are drawn behind its picture, but cannot stand on its tile.

## How planted trees differ from wild ones

| | Planted tree | Wild tree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grows overnight | Never | 1 in 5 chance a night, up to Huge |
| Logs when felled | None | Its size's logs, plus the Axe's extra |
| Counts toward the wild tree limit | No | Yes |
| Can be moved | Yes, keeping its size | No |
| Destroyed from its move menu | Returns <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its planting cost | |

Any Axe fells a planted tree, on any tile its picture covers.
