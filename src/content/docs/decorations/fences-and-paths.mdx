Fences are set down with the Decorate tool. Paths and grass are laid with the Terrain tool.

## Fences

A Wood Fence costs <Fact path="terrain.fenceCost" as="money" /> per tile and needs no materials. Fence pieces join to the fences next to them.

- A fence stands on bare grass only: not on water, farmland, a crop, a building, a rock, a tree or an animal, and not in front of an animal home.
- No animal can pass a fence. As animals eat only the crop they stand on, a fenced field is safe from them.
- Farmland and water cannot be laid on a fence's tile. Paths and grass can.
- A building more than one tile tall can stand over fences with its upper rows.

| To do this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Sell a placed fence with the Decorate tool | It returns its full price |
| Take it down with the Axe | One pass puts it in Storage as a Wood Fence. Storage needs a free slot. |
| Set a stored fence | Uses one from Storage and costs no gold |

## Paths

Every path is available from the start.

<DataTable name="paths" />

- Dirt Path and Brick Path each come in several looks. Pick the path in the Terrain tool on the tool bar, then tap the i button to open its Properties card and choose its look there. It remembers the last look you picked.
- Each tile keeps the look it was laid in.

- Paths are looks only: they change no speeds and animals walk on them like grass.
- A path replaces farmland or water on its tile, and removes the farmland's fertilizer.
- A path can be laid under a fence, a building or a decoration, and stays when the thing is removed.
- Laying a path of another kind over a path changes it.

## Grass

Grass is free. It clears a path, farmland or water from a tile back to plain grass, including a path under a fence, building or decoration. No terrain can be laid on a tile with a crop.
