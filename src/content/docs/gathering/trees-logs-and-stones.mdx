Wild trees are felled with the Axe for logs. Rocks are broken with the Pickaxe for stones (see [Mining](../mining/)). Logs and stones sell for nothing. Buildings and many decorations ask for them in [Storage](../../storage-and-selling/) as well as money.

## Wild trees

Wild trees are Pines and Oaks in four sizes: Small, Medium, Big and Huge. Each stands on the one tile under its trunk, and its picture rises above it.

<DataTable name="trees" where="wild" is="true" columns="Tree,Logs from a wild one" />

- A first Axe takes one pass for every log the tree gives, so a Huge tree takes more passes than a Small one.
- Any Axe fells any tree. A better Axe takes fewer passes and gives extra logs from wild trees.

| Axe level | Passes for each tree | Extra logs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One for each log it gives | None |
| 2 | Half as many, rounded up | 1 more from each wild tree |
| 3 | One | 2 more from each wild tree |

Upgrade prices and unlock levels are in [Tools](../../tools/).

## Felling a tree

1. Pick the Axe from the Harvest tool's first dropdown.
2. Drag the tool circle over the tree, or tap or swipe anywhere on its picture, leaves included. One pass is one go across the tree. Lift your finger or leave the tree and come back for the next pass.
3. A count and a bar above your finger show how close it is to falling.
4. On the last pass the tree falls and its logs go into Storage.

- If Storage cannot hold all the logs, the tree stays and says "Storage is full".
- Holding another implement over a tree says "Use the Axe from the Harvest tool to remove a tree". Tapping the banner picks the Axe up (see [Tools](../../tools/)).
- The Axe also takes down a fence or a decoration in one pass and stores it.

## Planted trees

Trees you plant from the Decorate tool give no logs when felled, whatever their size. They keep the size they were planted at and never grow. See [Planted Trees](../../decorations/planted-trees/).

## Logs and stones

- Logs and stones sell for nothing, so they have no Sell button in Storage.
- To get rid of them, tap **Trash** on them in Storage. Trash pays nothing.
- Buildings and decorations take logs and stones when you place them. Destroying one returns most of them (see [Placing and Moving](../../buildings/placing-and-moving/)).
- If you have too few, the game says "Need N logs in storage" or "Need N stones in storage" with the number missing.

## Overnight

The midnight wave grows wild trees and rocks and brings new ones. See [Wild Growth](../../world/wild-growth/).

- A wild tree smaller than Huge may grow one size if the ground around it is clear.
- Wild rocks may grow a size too, from Small to Medium to Big.
- New wild trees and rocks appear on empty ground anywhere outside the clearing around your first field.
- A farm short of trees or rocks gets more of them.
- Trees you planted and rocks you placed never grow.
