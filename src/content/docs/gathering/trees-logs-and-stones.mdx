Wild trees are felled with the Axe for logs, and rocks are broken with the Pickaxe for stones (see [Mining](../mining/)). Logs and stones sell for nothing. Buildings and many decorations need them from [Storage](../../storage-and-selling/) as well as gold.

## Wild trees

Wild trees are Pines and Oaks in four sizes: Small, Medium, Big and Huge. Each stands on the one tile under its trunk, and its picture rises above it.

<DataTable name="trees" where="wild" is="true" columns="Tree,Logs from a wild one" />

| Axe level | Passes for each tree | Extra logs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One for each log it gives | None |
| 2 | Half as many, rounded up | 1 more from each wild tree |
| 3 | One | 2 more from each wild tree |

- One pass is one stroke across any part of the tree's picture, leaves included. Lift your finger or leave the tree and come back for the next pass.
- A tree falls only if Storage can hold all its logs. Otherwise it stays.
- Any Axe fells any tree. Upgrade prices and levels are in [Tools](../../tools/).
- The Axe also takes a fence or a decoration down into Storage in one pass.

## Planted trees

Trees you plant from the Decorate tool give no logs when felled, whatever their size, and never grow. See [Planted Trees](../../decorations/planted-trees/).

## Logs and stones

- Logs and stones sell for nothing. They can be trashed from Storage for nothing.
- Buildings and decorations take them when placed, and destroying one returns <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of them (see [Buildings](../../buildings/#destroying)).
- The tutorial's Pet House step adds small pines, small rocks or a Copper Vein near you if the farm has none of the first-level kind to gather.

## Overnight

The midnight wave grows wild trees and brings new ones. See [Wild Growth](../../world/wild-growth/).

- Each wild tree smaller than Huge has a 1 in 5 chance each night to grow one size, if the bigger tree's picture has bare grass to spread over.
- New wild trees appear on empty grass anywhere outside the clearing around the starting farmland and the two tiles in front of the cave entrance, until the farm has 390 wild trees.
- A farm short of trees, or with too few logs in Storage for its next buildings, gets more of them.
- Trees you planted never grow and do not count toward the limit.
