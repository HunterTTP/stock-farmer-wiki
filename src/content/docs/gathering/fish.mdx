Fish are caught with the Fishing Rod (see [Fishing](../fishing/)). Every kind of fish has one of five rarities, which sets when it can appear, how often, how hard it is to catch, what it sells for and how much XP it earns.

## Rarities

<DataTable name="rarities" />

<DataTable name="fish-rarity-details" />

- The odds of each rarity at each level are on [Fishing](../fishing/#which-fish-appear). Within a rarity, every kind is equally likely, so a rarity with fewer kinds gives each of them more often.
- Common fish are the small pond fish, Uncommon the bigger freshwater fish, Rare the small, exotic and sea fish, Epic the larger sea fish and Legendary the giants.
- Butterflies and fireflies use the same rarity colors (see [Butterflies and Fireflies](../butterflies-and-fireflies/)).

## All fish

<DataTable name="fish" />

- Weight is the range in pounds a fish of that kind can have. Most are near the middle (see [Fishing](../fishing/#weight)).
- "Sells for" is what the lightest and the heaviest fish of that kind fetch: <Fact path="fish.lightestPricePercent" />% of its rarity's price at the lightest weight, up to <Fact path="fish.heaviestPricePercent" />% at the heaviest.
- The price is the same when the fish is sold later from [Storage](../../storage-and-selling/#fish).
- Every fish can also be fed to an animal (see [Feeding](../../animals/feeding/)).
