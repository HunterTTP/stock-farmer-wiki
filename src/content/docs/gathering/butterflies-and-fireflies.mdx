Butterflies fly by day and fireflies come out at night. Both are caught with the Bucket and kept in [Storage](../../storage-and-selling/). They cannot be sold. A caught one can be set free on the farm again.

## Butterflies

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| When | Full daylight, no rain, every season except Winter |
| Chance | 10% each second that one flies in from the edge of the map, while there are fewer than 10 |
| Most on the farm | 10, counting ones you set free |
| Shifting wings | 1% |
| Each of Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary | 19.8% |

- A butterfly visits a couple of crops, rocks or decorations, rests on each for a few seconds and leaves again. With nothing to visit, it crosses the farm.
- Shifting wings slowly go round all the hues.
- With 10 set free on the farm, no wild ones come out.

## Fireflies

Each night has exactly <Fact path="world.firefliesPerNight" /> fireflies.

- The night from 9 PM to 4 AM is cut into ten equal parts, and one firefly comes out of the forest at a random moment in each part.
- Three of the ten, picked at random, glow in a color. The rest are the usual warm orange.
- Every night is the same however often you open the farm.

| Glow of a colored firefly | Chance | Chance a night has at least one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uncommon | 44.4% | 83% |
| Rare | 27.8% | 62% |
| Epic | 15.6% | 40% |
| Legendary (deep orange) | 8.9% | 24% |
| Shifting | 3.3% | 10% |

- There are no white Common fireflies.
- A firefly whose moment passes while the game is closed is missed.
- At most 10 fireflies are on the farm at once, counting ones you set free. One that comes out when the farm is full waits for room, and is missed if none opens before the night ends.
- Fireflies fade out as day comes and are gone by morning.

## Catching

- Only the Bucket catches them. A catch goes into Storage as a Butterfly of its wing color (such as "Rare Butterfly"), a "Firefly" (orange) or a colored firefly (such as "Epic Firefly").
- Storage must have a free slot.
- Each wing color and each glow is one entry in [Progress](../../progress/), counted when you catch it.

## XP

> XP = XP to leave your level ÷ 600 × multiplier, rounded, at least 1

| Wings or glow | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Common butterfly, orange firefly | 1 |
| Uncommon | 2 |
| Rare | 4 |
| Epic | 7 |
| Legendary | 10 |
| Shifting | 15 |

- XP is earned only for a wild one. Catching one you set free earns nothing and is not counted in Progress.
- No XP is earned at the top level.
- A Common butterfly earns half what an average Common fish does (see [Fishing](../fishing/#xp-for-fish)).

## Setting them free

- A butterfly or firefly set free from Storage flutters within 1 tile of where you let it go, diagonals included, until you catch it again.
- At most 10 butterflies and 10 fireflies can be set free at once.
- Fireflies can only be set free at night. Set-free fireflies fade out by day and come back the next night.
- A set-free butterfly rests nearby while it is dark, raining or snowing, and flutters again when it is fine.
- Releasing one from Storage instead throws it away for nothing.
