Butterflies fly by day and fireflies come out at night. Both are caught with the Bucket, one of the [Harvest implements](../../tools/), and kept in [Storage](../../storage-and-selling/). They cannot be sold. A caught one can be let go on the farm again.

## Butterflies

- Butterflies fly in from the edge of the map in dry daylight, in every season except Winter.
- Each visits a couple of crops, rocks or decorations, rests on them for a few seconds and leaves again. With nothing to visit, it crosses the farm.
- Its wings are one of the five rarity colors, each equally likely: Common (white), Uncommon (green), Rare (blue), Epic (purple) or Legendary (orange). Very rarely, the wings are Shifting, a color that slowly goes round all the hues.
- Wild butterflies and ones you released together never number more than 10 on the farm. With 10 released, no wild ones come out.

### Catching a butterfly

1. Pick the Bucket from the Harvest tool's first dropdown.
2. Tap a butterfly, or drag the tool circle over the tile it is flying over. The tile shows green. Other implements pass through butterflies.
3. It goes into Storage as a Butterfly of its wing color, such as "Rare Butterfly". Storage must have room, or the game says "Storage is full".

Each wing color is one entry in [Progress](../../progress/). A butterfly counts when you catch it, not when you see it.

## Fireflies

- Each night has exactly <Fact path="world.firefliesPerNight" /> fireflies. They come out of the forest one at a time between 9 pm and 4 am and drift in toward the middle of the farm.
- Three of each night's fireflies are colored and the rest are the usual warm orange. Every night is the same however often you open the farm.
- A colored firefly glows Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Shifting. Uncommon is the most likely and Shifting the rarest. There are no white Common fireflies. A Legendary one glows a deep orange.
- A firefly whose moment passes while the farm is closed is missed. Never more than 10 fireflies are on the farm at once, counting ones you set free, so one that comes out when the farm is full waits for room and is missed if none opens before the night ends.
- Fireflies fade out as day comes and are gone by morning.

### Catching a firefly

Catch a firefly with the Bucket the same way as a butterfly: tap it or drag over it while it shows. It goes into Storage as a "Firefly" (orange) or a colored one such as "Epic Firefly". Each glow has its own entry in [Progress](../../progress/).

## XP

- A butterfly or firefly earns XP once, when you catch a wild one.
- The amount follows the XP to leave your level, the same share at every level.
- No XP is earned at the top level.

| Wings or glow | XP compared with a Common butterfly |
| --- | --- |
| Common, and an ordinary orange firefly | 1× |
| Uncommon | 2× |
| Rare | 4× |
| Epic | 7× |
| Legendary | 10× |
| Shifting | 15× |

A Common butterfly earns half the XP of an average Common fish at the same level (see [Fishing](../fishing/)). Catching one you let go earns nothing and is not counted in Progress.

## Letting them go

- In Storage, **Release** on a butterfly or firefly throws it away for nothing.
- To set one free on the farm, tap **Select** on it in Storage. The Decorate tool now holds it. Tap a tile to let it go there.
- A set-free butterfly or firefly flutters within 1 tile of where you let it go, in every direction including diagonals, until you catch it again.
- At most 10 butterflies can be released at once ("At most 10 butterflies can be released on the farm"), and at most 10 fireflies can be set free.
- Fireflies can only be set free at night ("Fireflies can only be set free at night"). Set-free fireflies fade out by day and are back the next night.
- A released butterfly rests on a nearby spot while it is dark, raining or snowing, and flutters again when it is fine.
