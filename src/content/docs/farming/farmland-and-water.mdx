Crops grow only on farmland. Farmland, water and grass are laid with the Terrain tool.

## Terrain

| Terrain | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farmland | <Fact path="terrain.farmlandCost" as="money" /> | Soil for crops. Limited by level. |
| Water | <Fact path="terrain.waterCost" as="money" /> | Unlimited. Deep, Medium and Shallow are looks only. Refills the Watering Can. Big enough water holds fish and ducks. |
| Grass | Free | Clears farmland, water and paths back to grass. |

- Any terrain replaces another at once. Farmland over water removes the water, and water over farmland removes the farmland.
- Water, paths and grass laid over farmland also remove its fertilizer.
- Terrain is refused on a tile with a crop, ripe or growing, or an egg.
- Terrain is refused under a rock, a tree, a fence, a building or a decoration. Paths and grass are the exception: they can go under or out from under a fence, building or decoration.
- Water cannot go on the tile in front of an animal home.
- Laying water in a new look changes every water tile already there.
- Water holds fish and ducks when it has a tile with water on all eight sides (see [Fishing](../../gathering/fishing/#where-fish-live) and [Ducks](../../animals/ducks/)).
- Ponds refill the Watering Can when it is below full, and do not water nearby farmland on their own.

## Farmland limit

The number of farmland tiles you can have at once rises with your level. You start with <Fact path="world.startingFarmland" />.

| Level reached | Tiles added |
| --- | --- |
| <Fact path="terrain.farmlandFirstUnlockLevel" /> to <Fact path="terrain.farmlandDoubleToLevel" /> | Twice the level (level 8 adds 16) |
| <Fact path="terrain.farmlandSingleFromLevel" /> to <Fact path="world.maxLevel" /> | The level (level 25 adds 25) |

The total for every level is in the [level table](../../levels/#level-table). At the limit, more farmland is refused. Erasing farmland or covering it with water frees room.

## Watering and growth

Watered soil grows crops <Fact path="terrain.waterSpeedUpPercent" />% faster (<Fact path="terrain.waterMultiplier" />× speed), while dry crops keep growing at normal speed.

- Hand-watered and rain-watered farmland stays watered for 1 farm day (until the next farm midnight).
- Afternoon rain in Spring, Summer and Fall automatically waters all farmland for the day (see [Weather](../../world/weather/)). Winter snow never waters farmland.
- A [Sprinkler](../fertilizer-and-sprinklers/) keeps the farmland in its reach watered every day, whether or not it is spraying.
- Farmland watered by several sources gets the boost once. The boost does not add up.
- The boost multiplies with fertilizer, a Scarecrow and a Greenhouse (see [Growth](../growth/)).

## Watering Can

The Watering Can is one of the Harvest implements (see [Tools and Upgrades](../../tools/)). Tap or drag it over empty farmland or growing crops to water them, and tap or drag over any pond to refill it when it is below full.

| Level | Unlock | Cost | Capacity | Area |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Start | Free | 12 tiles | 1×1 |
| Level 2 | Level 4 | 200 gold | 24 tiles | 1×1 |
| Level 3 | Level 7 | 900 gold | 48 tiles | 3×3 splash |

- Watering an already-watered tile uses no water from the can.
- Tapping or dragging the Watering Can on a pond refills it only when it is below full.
- In the farm tutorial, a short Watering phase follows planting Carrots to introduce the Watering Can.

### Sprinkler soaking

- Farmland around a newly placed or moved Sprinkler soaks up water one tile at a time, in a random order, about <Fact path="terrain.soakingPaceSeconds" /> s apart.
- A large area soaks a little faster, so it finishes within <Fact path="terrain.longestSoakingSeconds" as="duration" />.
- Farmland that loses its last Sprinkler dries out the same way, tile by tile, and keeps the boost until its tile dries.
