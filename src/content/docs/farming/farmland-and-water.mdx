Crops grow only on farmland. Farmland, water and grass are placed with the Terrain tool.

## The Terrain tool

The Terrain tool holds several kinds of terrain and remembers the one you last used. Tap a tile or drag across several to apply it.

| Terrain | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farmland | <Fact path="terrain.farmlandCost" as="money" /> | Soil for crops. Limited by level. |
| Water | Free | Water tiles. Unlimited. Comes in Deep, Medium and Shallow. |
| Grass | Free | Erases farmland, water and paths back to grass. |

Paths and trees are also in this tool (see [Fences and Paths](../../decorations/fences-and-paths/) and [Planted Trees](../../decorations/planted-trees/)).

- Any terrain can be placed over another and replaces it at once. Farmland over water removes the water, and water over farmland removes the farmland.
- Water, paths and grass placed over farmland also remove its fertilizer.
- Terrain is refused on a tile with a crop on it, ripe or growing. Harvest or destroy the crop first.
- Terrain is refused where a rock, a tree, a fence, a building or a decoration stands. Paths and grass are the exception: they can go under or out from under a fence, building or decoration. A sprinkler stands on any terrain.
- Water cannot go in front of an animal home.
- Placing water in a new color changes the tiles already there.
- Big ponds hold fish (see [Fishing](../../gathering/fishing/)).

## Farmland limit

The number of farmland tiles you can have at once rises with your level. You start with <Fact path="world.startingFarmland" /> tiles. Each level adds more:

- Levels 2 to 19 add twice the level number in tiles (level 8 adds 16).
- Level 20 and above add as many tiles as the level number (level 25 adds 25).

The full list is in the [level table](../../levels/#level-table). At the limit, placing farmland says "Farmland limit reached". Erasing farmland or covering it with water frees room.

## Water and growth

Farmland next to water is saturated. Saturated soil grows crops 5% faster.

- A water tile saturates the farmland within 1 tile of it, diagonals included.
- A [Sprinkler](../fertilizer-and-sprinklers/) saturates farmland within its reach, whether or not it is spraying.
- Land near several sources is saturated once, not more.
- The boost stacks with fertilizer and scarecrows (see [Growth](../growth/)).

### Soaking

- Farmland soaks up water one tile at a time, in a random order, about half a second apart.
- It happens when water, a sprinkler or farmland is placed, moved or removed.
- A crowded area soaks a little faster, so it is done within 12 seconds.
- Land that loses its last water source dries out the same way.
- Only a tile that has soaked up water gets the boost. The farmland picture changes to show it.
