A crop starts growing when it is planted and takes its grow time from the [crops table](../crops/#all-crops), divided by the boosts on its tile.

## Real time

- Crops grow on the real clock, also while the game is closed. When you come back, everything that finished is ripe.
- Seasons and the time of day never change grow time. Afternoon rain in Spring, Summer and Fall waters all farmland for the day (see [Farmland and Water](../farmland-and-water/)).
- [Fruit trees](../fruit-trees/) grow the same way, start again each time they are picked, and receive the Greenhouse boost while a Greenhouse stands on the farm.

## Boosts

| Boost | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watered soil | <Fact path="terrain.waterSpeedUpPercent" />% faster | Watered by the Watering Can, afternoon rain or a Sprinkler (see [Farmland and Water](../farmland-and-water/)) |
| Fertilizer | <Fact path="terrain.fertilizerSpeedUpPercent" />% faster | On the tile, until its crop is harvested (see [Fertilizer and Sprinklers](../fertilizer-and-sprinklers/)) |
| Scarecrow | <Fact path="terrain.scarecrowSpeedUpPercent" />% faster | Within <Fact path="terrain.scarecrowReach" /> tiles of a Scarecrow |
| Greenhouse | 15% faster | Across the whole farm (crops and fruit trees) while placed (see [Buildings](../../buildings/#greenhouse)) |

> Grow time on a tile = base grow time ÷ (<Fact path="terrain.waterMultiplier" /> if watered) ÷ (<Fact path="terrain.fertilizerMultiplier" /> if fertilized) ÷ (<Fact path="terrain.scarecrowMultiplier" /> near a Scarecrow) ÷ (1.15 with a Greenhouse)

- Watered soil, fertilizer and a Scarecrow together make a crop grow about <Fact path="terrain.allBoostsMultiplier" /> times as fast, so it takes about <Fact path="terrain.allBoostsTimeSavedPercent" />% less time. Adding a Greenhouse stacks on top for about 2.72× speed (about 63.2% less time).
- Each boost counts once per tile, however many sources reach it.
- Boosts apply continuously while active. Watering a tile, placing a Sprinkler, adding a Scarecrow or building a Greenhouse speeds up crops already growing, and removing a boost returns them to normal speed without losing progress.

### Example

Tomato has a base grow time of <Fact path="crops.tomato.growSeconds" as="duration" />.

| Boosts on the tile | Ripe after |
| --- | --- |
| None | 22 min 9 s |
| Watered soil | 14 min 46 s |
| Fertilizer | 14 min 46 s |
| Watered soil and fertilizer | 9 min 51 s |
| Watered soil, fertilizer and Scarecrow | 9 min 23 s |
| Watered soil, fertilizer, Scarecrow and Greenhouse | 8 min 9 s |

## Pets ripening crops

A cat or dog standing on a growing crop may make it ripen at once. The longer it stays on crops, the likelier it is.

<DataTable name="pet-crop-rush" />

- The time counts up while the pet walks from one crop tile to the next, and starts again from zero once it steps off farmland with a crop.
- About 1 in 5 pets ripen a crop within their first <Fact path="animals.pets.cropRushCapSeconds" as="duration" /> on crops, and about half within 30 s.
- A Well Fed pet counts each second as <Fact path="animals.meal.speedMultiplier" /> s, so its chance is <Fact path="animals.meal.speedUpPercent" />% higher, up to <Fact path="animals.pets.cropRushWellFedMaxPercent" />% a second (see [Feeding](../../animals/feeding/)).
- It works only on a growing crop, never on a fruit tree or a ripe crop, and never while the pet is inside its house at night or in heavy rain.
- It happens only while the game is open.

## Animals eating crops

Grown chickens and farm animals out in the open sometimes eat the crop they stand on.

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Who eats | Grown hens, roosters and farm animals that are outside and not Well Fed |
| Who never eats | Pets, ducks, chicks, babies, animals inside their home |
| What | The whole crop on the tile it stands on, growing or ripe. Never a fruit tree. |
| Chance | 1 in <Fact path="animals.grazing.biteOddsDenominator" /> (about <Fact path="animals.grazing.biteChancePercent" />%) for each second it stands on a crop |
| Most often | One crop per animal every <Fact path="animals.grazing.restSeconds" as="duration" /> of real time |

- An animal on a crop has about a 50% chance of eating it within 20 s, and about 87% within a minute.
- The eaten crop gives no gold or XP and counts toward no quest. The farmland stays and its fertilizer is gone.
- The crop feeds the animal like a meal by hand: it is Well Fed for <Fact path="animals.meal.seconds" as="duration" /> (see [Feeding](../../animals/feeding/#eating-crops-in-the-field)), so it leaves crops alone for that time too.
- An animal eats only the tile it stands on, so a fence around a field keeps every animal out (see [Fences and Paths](../../decorations/fences-and-paths/)).
- It happens only while the game is open.
