A crop starts growing when it is planted and takes its grow time from the [crops table](../crops/#all-crops). Growth runs on the real clock.

## Real time

- Crops keep growing while the app is closed. When you come back, everything that finished is ripe.
- Grow time counts from the moment of planting. Seasons, weather and the time of day do not change it.
- Each crop shows four small plants that ripen one after another (see [Crops](../crops/#four-plants-per-tile)).
- Tapping a growing crop shows how much time is left.
- [Fruit trees](../fruit-trees/) grow the same way, and start again each time they are picked.

## Boosts

Three things make a farmland tile grow crops faster.

| Boost | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saturated soil | 5% faster | Within 1 tile of water, or in reach of a sprinkler (see [Farmland and Water](../farmland-and-water/)) |
| Fertilizer | <Fact path="terrain.fertilizerSpeedUpPercent" />% faster | On the tile, until the crop is harvested (see [Fertilizer and Sprinklers](../fertilizer-and-sprinklers/)) |
| Scarecrow | 5% faster | Within 3 tiles of a Scarecrow |

- The boosts multiply: a tile with all three grows 1.05 × 1.1 × 1.05, about 1.21 times as fast.
- A boost works on the crop as the farm is now. A sprinkler or Scarecrow placed later also speeds crops that are already growing.
- Fruit trees stand on grass, so no boost applies to them.

### Example

Tomato has a base grow time of <Fact path="crops.tomato.growSeconds" as="duration" />. Planted on a tile with these boosts, it is ripe after:

| Boosts on the tile | Ripe after |
| --- | --- |
| None | 22 min 9 s |
| Saturated soil | 21 min 6 s |
| Fertilizer | 20 min 9 s |
| Saturated soil and fertilizer | 19 min 11 s |
| Saturated soil, fertilizer and Scarecrow | 18 min 16 s |

## Pets hurrying crops

- A pet standing on a growing crop can make it ripen at once. The longer it stays, the likelier it is.
- It works on crops only. It does not work on fruit trees or while the pet is in its house.
- A pet that has been fed by hand ripens crops faster for its five minutes.
- See [Pets](../../animals/pets/).

## Animals eating crops

Chickens and farm animals sometimes eat crops.

- Only a fully grown chicken or farm animal out in the open eats a crop. It must be standing on the crop's tile, growing or ripe.
- Each second on a crop gives it a small chance (about 1 in 30) of eating the whole tile.
- Each animal eats at most one crop a day on the farm's clock (<Fact path="world.realSecondsPerFarmDay" as="duration" />).
- The eaten crop gives no money or XP and counts toward no quest. The farmland stays, and the fertilizer on it is gone.
- The animal is fed by the crop like a meal by hand: Well Fed for its five minutes (see [Feeding](../../animals/feeding/#eating-crops-in-the-field)). A Well Fed animal leaves crops alone.
- Pets, ducks, chicks, babies and animals inside their home leave crops alone. Fruit trees are safe.
- A fence around a field keeps animals out (see [Fences and Paths](../../decorations/fences-and-paths/)).
