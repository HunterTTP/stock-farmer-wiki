Three things speed up crops: fertilizer, water from a sprinkler and a Scarecrow. How they combine is on [Growth](../growth/).

## Fertilizer

Fertilizer makes the crop on its tile grow <Fact path="terrain.fertilizerSpeedUpPercent" />% faster.

| | Fertilizer |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | <Fact path="terrain.fertilizerCost" as="money" /> per tile, or one Fertilizer from Storage |
| Goes on | Farmland with no crop on it, and not already fertilized |
| Lasts | One crop: it is used up when that crop is harvested |

- Fertilize a tile before planting it. A planted tile cannot be fertilized.
- Destroying a crop leaves the fertilizer on the tile for the next one.
- Fertilizer is lost when water, a path or grass is laid over the tile, or when an animal eats the crop (see [Growth](../growth/#animals-eating-crops)).
- Fertilizer on a tile with no crop can be thrown away for no money back.
- Rabbits, pigs and donkeys give Fertilizer (see [Livestock](../../animals/livestock/)). Each one spread from Storage fertilizes one tile and costs no money.

## Sprinklers

A sprinkler saturates the farmland around it, the same as farmland next to water (see [Farmland and Water](../farmland-and-water/)), so crops there grow <Fact path="terrain.waterSpeedUpPercent" />% faster.

| | Sprinkler | Big Sprinkler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.cost" as="money" /> | <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.cost" as="money" /> |
| Materials | <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.materials.Copper Ore.count" /> Copper Ore, <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.materials.Stones.count" /> Stones | <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.materials.Copper Ore.count" /> Copper Ore, <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.materials.Stones.count" /> Stones |
| Does | <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.effect" /> | <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.effect" /> |

- No level is needed, only money and materials from Storage.
- Reach is a square around the sprinkler, diagonals included.
- A sprinkler stands on one tile of any terrain.
- The boost lasts as long as the sprinkler stands, whether or not it is spraying. Land near several sprinklers or ponds is saturated once.
- A moved sprinkler waters its new land, and its old land dries out tile by tile.
- Destroying a sprinkler returns <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its money and materials.

### Spray schedule

The spray is a picture: it never changes the boost.

- A sprinkler sprays as soon as it is placed, and the land in reach soaks up the water during that first spray.
- After that every sprinkler sprays together once every <Fact path="world.sprinklerSprayEverySeconds" as="duration" /> (an hour of the farm's clock), for 12 s each time.
- Sprinklers do not spray while it rains.
- A sprinkler set to Always On sprays all the time, rain or not.

## Scarecrow

A Scarecrow is a decoration that makes crops near it grow 5% faster.

| | Scarecrow |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.cost" as="money" /> |
| Materials | <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.materials.Wheat.count" /> Wheat, <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.materials.Logs.count" /> Logs |
| Does | <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.effect" /> |

- Reach is a square around it, diagonals included.
- Several scarecrows covering the same tile give the boost once.
