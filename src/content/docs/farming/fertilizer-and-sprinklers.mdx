Three things speed up crops: fertilizer, water from a sprinkler and a Scarecrow. The Fertilize tool holds fertilizer, the Sprinkler and the Big Sprinkler, and remembers which one you last used.

## Fertilizer

Fertilizer makes the crop planted on a tile grow <Fact path="terrain.fertilizerSpeedUpPercent" />% faster.

1. Open the Fertilize tool and pick Fertilizer.
2. Tap an empty farmland tile, or drag across several. Each tile costs <Fact path="terrain.fertilizerCost" as="money" />.
3. Plant a crop on the tile.

- Fertilizer goes on farmland with no crop on it. A tile that already has fertilizer says "Already fertilized".
- It is used up when the crop on the tile is harvested, so its boost lasts for one crop. Destroying a crop leaves the fertilizer on the tile.
- It is also lost when water, a path or grass is placed over the tile, or when an animal eats the crop (see [Growth](../growth/#animals-eating-crops)).
- To throw fertilizer away, hold a finger on a fertilized tile that has no crop and confirm. No money is returned.
- Fertilizer collected from animals (see [Livestock](../../animals/livestock/)) can be placed from Storage. Pick it in the Storage menu, then tap or drag over farmland. It uses up one fertilizer per tile and costs no money.

## Sprinklers

A sprinkler waters the farmland around it. Watered farmland is saturated, the same as farmland next to water (see [Farmland and Water](../farmland-and-water/)), and crops on it grow 5% faster.

| | Sprinkler | Big Sprinkler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.cost" as="money" /> | <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.cost" as="money" /> |
| Materials | <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.materials.Copper Ore.count" /> Copper Ore, <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.materials.Stones.count" /> Stones | <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.materials.Copper Ore.count" /> Copper Ore, <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.materials.Stones.count" /> Stones |
| Does | <Fact path="decorations.sprinkler.effect" /> | <Fact path="decorations.bigSprinkler.effect" /> |

- No level is needed. The materials come from Storage (see [Mining](../../gathering/mining/) and [Trees, Logs and Stones](../../gathering/trees-logs-and-stones/)). Without them the game says how many Copper Ore you need.
- Reach is counted in a square around the sprinkler, diagonals included.
- Place it like a building: pick it in the Fertilize tool, tap a tile, adjust it, then Place. It stands on any terrain.
- Destroying a sprinkler returns <Fact path="terrain.refundPercent" />% of its money and materials. The land it watered dries out tile by tile.
- A moved sprinkler waters its new land.
- Land near several sprinklers or ponds is saturated once. The boost does not add up.

### Spray

The boost does not depend on the spray. It is there while the sprinkler stands.

- A sprinkler sprays as soon as it is placed. After that, every sprinkler sprays together once every <Fact path="world.sprinklerSprayEverySeconds" as="duration" />, an hour of the farm's clock.
- A spray lasts 12 seconds and falls on every tile in reach.
- Sprinklers do not spray while it rains.
- Land in reach soaks up the water tile by tile during the first spray.
- Always On is a switch on each sprinkler's page and in the menu when you tap a placed sprinkler. With it on, every sprinkler sprays all the time like a fountain, rain or not. It changes only how the farm looks.

## Scarecrow

A Scarecrow makes crops near it grow 5% faster. It is a decoration, placed with the Decorate tool.

| | Scarecrow |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.cost" as="money" /> |
| Materials | <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.materials.Wheat.count" /> Wheat, <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.materials.Logs.count" /> Logs |
| Does | <Fact path="decorations.scarecrow.effect" /> |

- Reach is a square around it, diagonals included.
- Several scarecrows covering the same tile give the boost once.

## How boosts combine

Each boost that applies to a tile multiplies the growth speed. A tile with all three grows 1.05 × 1.1 × 1.05 = about 1.21 times as fast, so a crop takes about 17.5% less time. See [Growth](../growth/) for an example.
