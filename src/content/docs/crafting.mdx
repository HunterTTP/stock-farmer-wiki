[Workshops](../buildings/workshops/) turn goods from storage into goods worth more. A recipe takes a number of one ingredient and gives one or more of its product after a set time.

## All recipes

<DataTable name="recipes" />

- **From** is taken from storage when you tap Start.
- **Each sells for** is the sale price of one item made.
- **Unlock level** is the player level at which the recipe can be chosen. It is the level of the workshop, the crop or the ore, whichever is highest.
- The first recipe of each workshop is available as soon as the workshop is built.
- Collect finished goods with the Bucket. See [Workshops](../buildings/workshops/).

## How artisan goods are priced

Flour, Bread, Planks, Cloth, Bricks and Cheese have set prices. Every other good is an artisan good, and its price comes from what goes into it:

- An artisan good sells for 1.5 times the sale price of everything that goes in, rounded to cents.
- A bar or a polished gem sells for 2 times the sale price of the ore that goes in.

Bakes bread, cooks fruit and vegetables, roasts coffee and smelts ore into bars.

<DataTable name="recipes" where="building" is="Stone Oven" />

- Preserves take baskets from fruit trees.
- Bars take ore from [Mining](../gathering/mining/). Ore recipes unlock at the level at which ore can be smelted.
