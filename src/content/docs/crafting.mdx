[Workshops](../buildings/workshops/) turn goods from storage into goods worth more. A recipe takes a number of one ingredient and gives one or more of its product after a set time.

## All recipes

<DataTable name="recipes" />

- **From** is taken from Storage when the batch starts.
- **Each sells for** is the sale price of one item made.
- **Unlock level** is the level of the workshop or of the crop that goes in, whichever is higher. Bars open at level <Fact path="mining.processingLevel" />.
- Collect finished goods with the Bucket. Batch rules are on [Workshops](../buildings/workshops/).

## How artisan goods are priced

Flour, Bread, Planks, Cloth, Bricks and Cheese have set prices. Every other good is an artisan good, and its price comes from what goes into it:

> Artisan good price = sale price of one ingredient × number that go in × <Fact path="mining.artisanStandardMultiplier" />, rounded to whole silver

- A bar or a polished gem uses <Fact path="mining.artisanOreMultiplier" /> in place of <Fact path="mining.artisanStandardMultiplier" />, since ore is scarce.
- Recipes that make two items (Bread, Bricks, Cloth from Cotton) have set prices.
- A workshop recipe earns no XP. Its profit is the gap between the good's price and its ingredients'.
